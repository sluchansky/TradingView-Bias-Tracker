NEXT UPGRADE – PROFESSIONAL TRADE PLANNING

I want the AI Trading Partner to behave like a professional institutional analyst, not just a rule-based strategy checker. The AI should continuously analyze the market, think ahead, and explain what it is waiting for before taking a trade.

1. MARKET PHASE ANALYSIS

Add a "Market Phase" section that continuously classifies the current market.

Possible phases:
- Trend Beginning
- Trend Developing
- Trend Mature
- Trend Exhausted
- Pullback
- Consolidation
- Breakout
- Reversal Watch

Display the current phase along with a confidence percentage.

Example:

Market Phase: Trend Exhausted (94%)

Reason:
- Impulse leg is nearly complete.
- Price is significantly extended from VWAP.
- Selling now would likely be chasing the move.
- Risk-to-reward is poor until price retraces.

The Market Phase should update continuously as new data arrives.

------------------------------------------------------------

2. BETTER ENTRY PROBABILITY

Instead of only displaying the current Entry Quality score, estimate how much the trade quality would improve if the market changes.

Example:

Current Entry Quality:
33%

After Pullback to VWAP:
82%

At Fresh Supply Zone:
91%

After Bearish Rejection:
95%

This tells the trader why waiting may produce a significantly better trade.

------------------------------------------------------------

3. NEXT OPPORTUNITY PANEL

When a trade is rejected, do not stop the analysis.

Instead, immediately begin planning the next high-probability setup.

Display:

NEXT OPPORTUNITY

Current Market Bias:
Bullish / Bearish / Neutral

Waiting For:
- Pullback to VWAP
- Rejection Candle
- CVD Confirmation
- Delta Confirmation
- Fresh Supply/Demand Zone
- Structure Confirmation
- Volume Confirmation

Estimated Entry Price

Suggested Stop Loss

Suggested Take Profit

Projected Risk/Reward

Projected Confidence

Estimated Time Until Setup (if possible)

This section should update continuously until a READY signal is produced.

------------------------------------------------------------

4. PROFESSIONAL WAIT REASONING

Whenever the verdict is WAIT, explain the decision like a professional trader.

Instead of generic messages, explain exactly why.

Example:

Reason for WAIT:

Current price is overextended.

The impulse leg is nearly complete.

Entering now would mean chasing momentum.

Professional traders typically wait for a pullback before entering.

Waiting improves the probability while reducing risk.

------------------------------------------------------------

5. PROFESSIONAL ANALYST OUTLOOK

Add a completely new analysis section.

Professional Analyst Outlook

Answer questions such as:

- What is the market trying to accomplish?
- Who currently controls the market?
- Where is liquidity likely sitting?
- Where is the next high-probability entry?
- What would invalidate the current trade idea?
- What would immediately increase confidence?
- What would immediately cancel the setup?
- Is the AI expecting continuation or reversal?

This should read like a market analyst giving a live briefing.

------------------------------------------------------------

6. THINK AHEAD INSTEAD OF REACTING

The AI should no longer simply evaluate alerts.

It should continuously build a forward-looking market thesis.

Even when there is no trade, the AI should always explain:

- What it is waiting for.
- Why it is waiting.
- What conditions must occur before issuing READY.
- Where the next ideal entry is likely to occur.
- Whether patience is expected to improve the trade.
- Whether buyers or sellers are currently gaining or losing control.

The goal is to make the AI feel like an experienced trading partner who is always thinking several steps ahead instead of only reacting when an alert triggers.
