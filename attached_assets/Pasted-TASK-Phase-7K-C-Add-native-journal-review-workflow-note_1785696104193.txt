==================================================
PART 1 — REVIEW STATES
==================================================

Support canonical review statuses:

- UNREVIEWED
- IN_PROGRESS
- REVIEWED
- NEEDS_REVIEW
- EXCLUDED

Define exact transition rules.

Examples:

UNREVIEWED
→ IN_PROGRESS
→ REVIEWED

STATUS_UNKNOWN, missing strategy, or missing execution may force:
NEEDS_REVIEW

Operator may mark:
EXCLUDED

Every review-status change must record:

- timestamp
- prior status
- new status
- operator
- reason

Do not use review_status to modify lifecycle_status.

==================================================
PART 11 — LEARNING ELIGIBILITY BOUNDARY
==================================================

Review may unlock learning eligibility only when all existing non-review conditions already pass.

Review must never override:

- missing strategy
- missing planned risk
- duplicate
- unresolved execution
- ambiguous import
- invalid outcome
- status unknown

Define review rules by source.

Suggested:

SYSTEM_AUTO
- closed
- valid outcome
- no unresolved status
- review may be optional or required by configured policy

SYSTEM_MANUAL_CONFIRM
- review required

EXTERNAL_MANUAL
- review and strategy attribution required

TRADZELLA_IMPORT
- match confidence and review required

Do not change learning formulas.
