TASK: Left Brain Evolution — Phase 2 Market Memory & Dynamic Thesis

OBJECTIVE

The current Left Brain correctly describes the market at a single point in time.

Phase 2 upgrades it from a static observer into a dynamic market analyst.

A professional trader does not only know what the market is.

A professional trader knows:

• how it got here
• what has changed
• what strengthened the thesis
• what weakened the thesis
• what would invalidate the thesis

The Left Brain should now maintain a continuous evolving thesis.

This is NOT a new brain.

This is NOT another dashboard panel.

This is an evolution of the existing Left Brain Market Intelligence.

====================================================

PART 1 — MARKET MEMORY

Instead of only storing the current market state, maintain a rolling history of meaningful transitions.

Record only significant changes.

Do NOT save every bar.

Examples of events:

Market State changed

Session Character changed

Buyer/Seller Control changed materially

Directional Outlook changed materially

Playbook ranking changed

Data Confidence changed materially

Thesis strengthened

Thesis weakened

Thesis invalidated

Each event should include:

timestamp

previous state

new state

reason

supporting evidence

confidence at the time

Maintain a bounded history.

No unlimited growth.

====================================================

====================================================

PART 3 — THESIS STRENGTH

Maintain a separate score:

Thesis Strength

0–100

This is NOT edge score.

This is NOT confidence.

It represents how strongly the evidence supports the current market thesis.

Example:

Bearish Thesis

Strength

83

Increasing

====================================================

PART 4 — THESIS MOMENTUM

Track whether conviction is:

Increasing

Stable

Weakening

Reversing

This is separate from market direction.

A bearish thesis can weaken.

A bullish thesis can strengthen.

====================================================

PART 5 — THESIS EXPLANATION

Replace generic narrative with structured reasoning.

Example:

Current Thesis

Trend Day Down

Reason

Price remains below VWAP.

Seller delta remains dominant.

Lower highs continue.

Buyers have failed to reclaim value.

====================================================

PART 6 — WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND

Every thesis must include invalidation conditions.

Example

Current thesis favors bearish continuation.

This thesis weakens if:

• Buyers reclaim VWAP.

• Bullish BOS forms.

• Positive delta expands.

This thesis fails if:

• Market accepts above value.
