I want to consolidate the dashboard into one interactive “Main Brain” system.

Instead of having many separate analyst boxes, I want one large command-center panel that explains what the bot is seeing, thinking, waiting for, and planning to do.

Build it as a live scrolling conversation, not a report.

The Main Brain should constantly narrate the market in plain English:

- What price is doing
- What zones it is near
- Whether VWAP supports the idea
- Whether CVD/delta confirms
- Whether structure is bullish, bearish, or mixed
- Whether volatility is normal, elevated, or risky
- What trade idea is forming
- What is missing before READY
- Why it is saying WAIT
- What would invalidate the idea

I want it to feel like I am sitting beside a professional trader watching the chart.

Architecture:

Create four internal brain modules:

1. Market Brain
Watches trend, momentum, VWAP, CVD, liquidity, structure, zones, and volatility.

2. Strategy Brain
Decides whether the setup fits the playbook and whether the entry is clean or too late.

3. Risk Brain
Checks stop size, risk/reward, position size, volatility risk, daily loss risk, and whether the trade is worth taking.

4. Trade Manager
Only activates when I manually enter a trade or the bot enters a trade. It manages the trade after entry.

The Main Brain should combine those four opinions into one final live explanation.

Example:

Market Brain:
Price reclaimed VWAP and is holding above demand.

Strategy Brain:
Long setup is forming, but entry is not ready yet.

Risk Brain:
Risk is acceptable, but stop must stay below demand.

Main Brain:
I am watching for a pullback and confirmation candle. No entry yet.

Status:
BUILDING LONG

The Main Brain should use status badges:

WATCHING
BUILDING
READY
WAIT
MANAGING
INVALIDATED

Replace or remove redundant dashboard boxes like:
Ask the AI
Unified Analyst Report
Analyst Mode
Trade Debate
Professional Review
Entry Quality
Why Not Ready
AI Trade Checklist

Their information should now feed into the Main Brain instead of appearing as separate sections.

Keep only these main dashboard areas:

1. Main Brain
2. Final Verdict
3. Entry / Stop / Targets
4. Active Trade Management
5. Edge Score
6. Recent Alerts / Journal

For manual trades:

Add a place where I can enter:
- Symbol
- Long or short
- Entry price
- Stop loss
- Target
- Position size
- Why I entered

Once entered, Trade Manager should take over and tell me:
- Whether my entry is good or late
- Whether my stop makes sense
- When to take partials
- When to move stop to break even
- Whether to hold, reduce, or exit
- What invalidates the trade thesis

The goal is to make the dashboard feel less like scattered data boxes and more like one intelligent trading partner constantly explaining its reasoning.
