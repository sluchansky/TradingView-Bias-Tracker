TASK: AI Analyst Layer — Phase 2

OBJECTIVE

Build an AI Analyst layer on top of Decision Pipeline V2.

This layer does NOT make trading decisions.

It explains them.

The Analyst consumes the output of:

- OBSERVE
- INTERPRET
- PRIORITIZE
- VALIDATE
- DECIDE

and produces one concise market briefing.

==================================================
ANALYST RESPONSIBILITIES
==================================================

The Analyst must answer these questions in order:

1. What is happening?

Example:

"The market is currently Risk Off."

--------------------------------------------------

2. Why is it happening?

Examples:

- Geopolitical escalation
- Inflation concerns
- Fed policy expectations
- Strong earnings
- Energy supply disruption

--------------------------------------------------

3. Where is money flowing?

Examples:

Leading:

- Gold
- Energy
- Defense

Weak:

- Technology
- Consumer Discretionary

--------------------------------------------------

4. Which futures market is strongest?

Rank:

1. MGC
2. MNQ
3. MES
4. MYM

Explain WHY.

--------------------------------------------------

5. What does the technical engine think?

Reuse the existing production technical verdict.

Examples:

WAIT

APPROVE

REJECT

Do not recalculate.

--------------------------------------------------

6. What is preventing the trade?

Examples:

- Missing VWAP confirmation
- Fresh zone not tested
- Weak structure
- ATR too large
- Poor reward-to-risk

--------------------------------------------------

7. What must happen next?

Examples:

"Wait for a bullish reclaim above VWAP."

"Wait for a fresh demand-zone retest."

"Wait for structure confirmation."

--------------------------------------------------

8. What would invalidate this idea?

Examples:

- Loss of structure
- Break below demand
- VIX reversal
- Loss of safe-haven strength

==================================================
OUTPUT FORMAT
==================================================

The Analyst should produce ONE clean briefing.

Example:

--------------------------------------------------

Market Environment

Risk Off

Primary Driver

Geopolitical Escalation

Money Flow

Strong:

Gold
Energy
Defense

Weak:

Technology
Consumer

Highest Priority Market

MGC

Reason:

Safe-haven demand is stronger than equity momentum.

Technical Verdict

WAIT

Reason:

Price remains extended above VWAP.
A fresh demand-zone retest has not occurred.

Next Action

Wait for a valid pullback into structure.

Invalidation

Loss of bullish structure and sustained trade below VWAP.

--------------------------------------------------

REQUIREMENTS

Keep explanations concise.

Avoid repeating data already shown elsewhere.

Use plain English.

Do not generate trade commands.

Do not override production.

This layer is display-only.

It exists to make the AI easier for the trader to understand.
