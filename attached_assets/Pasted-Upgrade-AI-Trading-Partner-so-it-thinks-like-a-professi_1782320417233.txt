Upgrade AI Trading Partner so it thinks like a professional market analyst, not just a strategy trigger bot.

The bot should use an Analyst Reasoning Engine before allowing any trade.

Process every market update in this order:

1. Market Context
- What is the higher-timeframe bias?
- Is price trending, ranging, breaking out, or reverting?
- Is price near supply, demand, VWAP, prior high/low, liquidity, FVG, OB, or key session level?
- Is this a clean environment or choppy/noisy?

2. Trade Thesis
Before any READY signal, the bot must form a written thesis:
- Why would a long make sense?
- Why would a short make sense?
- Which side has the stronger argument?
- What would prove the thesis wrong?

3. Evidence Scoring
Score bullish and bearish evidence separately.
Examples:
Bullish evidence:
- reclaimed VWAP
- swept lows and reversed
- bullish CHOCH
- demand holding
- positive delta/CVD
- higher lows

Bearish evidence:
- rejected VWAP
- swept highs and failed
- bearish CHOCH
- supply holding
- negative delta/CVD
- lower highs

The bot should not trade just because one strategy fires.
It should trade only when the full evidence stack supports the same direction.

4. Risk and Location Check
Before READY, confirm:
- entry is not chasing
- stop is logical, not random
- target has room before opposing supply/demand
- risk/reward is acceptable
- trade is not entering directly into resistance/support
- volatility supports the setup

5. Professional Verdict
Every output must include:
- Market Bias
- Analyst Thesis
- Bull Case
- Bear Case
- Best Trade Idea
- Invalidation
- Why Not Trade
- Confidence
- Final Verdict: READY LONG, READY SHORT, WAIT, or NO TRADE

Important rule:
The bot must be allowed to say WAIT even if alerts fire, if the analyst reasoning does not support the trade.

Also add an “Analyst Mode” section to the dashboard showing:
- Current Market Story
- What Professionals Are Watching
- Best Setup Forming
- Trade Location Quality
- Risk Quality
- Reason For Waiting
- What Needs To Happen Next

Do not remove existing strategies or alerts.
Use alerts as evidence, not commands.
The bot should think in layers:
Context → Thesis → Evidence → Risk → Verdict.
