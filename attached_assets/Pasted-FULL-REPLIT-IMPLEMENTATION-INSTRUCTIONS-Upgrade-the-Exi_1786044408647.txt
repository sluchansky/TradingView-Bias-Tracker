4. Actual 9:30 ORB algorithm

The default strategy is a 10-minute Opening Range Breakout.

Use:

Time zone: America/New_York
Opening-range start: 09:30:00
Opening-range end: 09:40:00
Opening-range bars included: 09:30 through 09:39
Range-lock time: 09:40:00
Entry-window start: 09:40:00
Entry-window end: 10:30:00

At exactly 9:40 AM, independently lock the opening range for:

* MGC
* MNQ
* MES
* MYM

For each instrument calculate:

ORB_HIGH = highest completed canonical one-minute bar high from 09:30 through 09:39
ORB_LOW = lowest completed canonical one-minute bar low from 09:30 through 09:39
ORB_MIDPOINT = (ORB_HIGH + ORB_LOW) / 2
ORB_WIDTH = ORB_HIGH - ORB_LOW

Do not include the 9:40 one-minute bar in the opening-range calculation.

The 9:40 bar may become the first breakout-confirmation bar.

Do not allow entries before the selected opening range is locked.

Do not modify a locked range later in the session.

14. Maximum chase rule

Do not enter after price has moved too far beyond the range.

Initial default:

Maximum chase distance = 25% of ORB width

For a long:

Maximum permitted entry =
ORB_HIGH + (ORB_WIDTH × 0.25)

For a short:

Minimum permitted entry =
ORB_LOW - (ORB_WIDTH × 0.25)

When price exceeds the maximum chase distance:

State = BREAKOUT_MISSED
Reason = PRICE_EXCEEDED_MAXIMUM_CHASE

Do not chase the move at market.

When CLOSE_AND_RETEST is enabled, continue monitoring for a permitted retest.

Make the maximum chase percentage configurable globally and per instrument.
