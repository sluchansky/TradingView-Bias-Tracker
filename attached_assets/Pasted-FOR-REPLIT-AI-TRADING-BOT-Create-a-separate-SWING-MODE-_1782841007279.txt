FOR REPLIT / AI TRADING BOT:

Create a separate SWING MODE strategy library.

Swing Mode should not act like Scalp Mode. It should analyze higher timeframes, wait patiently, and only take trades with room for larger moves.

Main Swing Mode Process:

1. Check monthly trend.
2. Check weekly trend.
3. Check daily structure.
4. Check 4H structure.
5. Identify major supply and demand zones.
6. Identify liquidity above and below price.
7. Check VWAP / anchored VWAP if available.
8. Check volume, RVOL, and momentum.
9. Confirm there is enough room to target at least 1:2, preferably 1:3 to 1:5.
10. Reject trades that are too close to major opposing supply/demand.

Swing Mode Strategies To Add:

1. Weekly Trend Pullback
Trade in the direction of the weekly trend after price pulls back into the 20 EMA, 50 EMA, or major demand/supply zone.

2. Break and Retest
Wait for a daily support/resistance break, then enter only after price retests and holds the broken level.

3. EMA Trend Rider
Use 20 EMA, 50 EMA, and 200 EMA alignment. Long when 20 > 50 > 200. Short when 20 < 50 < 200.

4. Weekly Supply/Demand Rotation
Enter when price reacts strongly from a weekly/monthly supply or demand zone and confirms with daily structure shift.

5. Momentum Breakout
Look for compression, ATR contraction, low volume, then expansion candle with volume confirmation.

6. Multi-Timeframe Alignment
Only approve swing trades when monthly, weekly, daily, and 4H trends agree.

7. Volume Profile Rotation
Use POC, VAH, VAL, HVN, and LVN. Trade acceptance above value for longs or below value for shorts.

8. Relative Strength
Prefer assets showing strength compared to benchmark. Example: NQ stronger than ES, Gold stronger than DXY.

9. ATR Trend Rider
Use 2x ATR stop. Trail winners with ATR instead of moving stop too tight too early.

10. Liquidity Sweep Reversal
Look for major high/low sweep, rejection candle, CHOCH/BOS confirmation, and volume support.

Rules:
90-100 = Elite swing setup
80-89 = High-quality swing setup
70-79 = Watchlist or reduced size
Below 70 = Reject

Risk Rules:
- Minimum acceptable swing trade target is 1:2.
- Preferred target is 1:3.
- Elite trend continuation can target 1:5 or more.
- Do not force 1:5 if market structure does not support it.
- Stop should be based on structure or ATR, not random points.
- Avoid trades where stop is too tight for the timeframe.

Trade Management:
- TP1 at 1:2 or first major liquidity target.
- Move stop only after structure confirms continuation.
- Do not move to breakeven too early.
- Trail remaining position using ATR or higher low / lower high structure.
- Reassess trade at each daily close.
- Exit if original thesis is invalidated.
- Exit if daily structure reverses against trade.
- Exit if price rejects hard from major opposing supply/demand.
