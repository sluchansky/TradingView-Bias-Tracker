Add a 9:30 AM Eastern Breakout Mode.

Goal:
Every trading day at 9:30 AM ET, the bot should prepare for a clean upside or downside breakout and target large asymmetric reward when the setup is strong.

Core idea:
At the 9:30 AM ET market open, the bot should not instantly enter. It should observe the opening range, identify whether price is breaking upside or downside, then only trade if the breakout has confirmation.

Breakout Mode Rules:

1. Time Trigger
- Activate at 9:30 AM ET.
- Focus on the first 5–15 minutes after open.
- Build an opening range from the first 1m, 3m, 5m, and 15m candles.
- Track opening high, opening low, VWAP, volume, candle strength, wick rejection, and liquidity sweeps.

2. Direction Detection
The bot should look for:
- Break above opening range high = possible long.
- Break below opening range low = possible short.
- Liquidity sweep above/below followed by reversal = possible trap/reversal trade.
- No clean break = WAIT.

3. Entry Confirmation
Only take trade if:
- Breakout candle closes beyond range.
- Volume/RVOL supports the move.
- Price is on correct side of VWAP.
- Structure confirms direction with BOS/CHOCH.
- No immediate supply/demand wall directly in front of entry.
- Candle does not enter after an extended push.
- Entry is not at the very top/bottom of the move.

4. Risk/Reward Logic
This mode should search for trades with potential up to 1:5 reward/risk.

Acceptable target plans:
- 1:2 minimum for normal setups.
- 1:3 preferred.
- 1:4 strong setup.
- 1:5 only for clean trend expansion days.

The bot does not need to force 1:5. It should dynamically decide if the market realistically supports 1:2, 1:3, 1:4, or 1:5.

5. Trade Management
- TP1 at 1R or 2R depending on volatility.
- Move stop to breakeven only after structure confirms continuation, not immediately.
- Trail runners behind higher lows for longs or lower highs for shorts.
- Exit early if breakout fails back inside opening range.
- Exit early if VWAP is reclaimed against the trade.
- Exit early if volume dies and candles stall.
- Let runners work when momentum is clean.

6. Failure Rules
Reject trade if:
- Breakout candle has huge wick rejection.
- Break happens directly into major supply/demand.
- Price is already overextended.
- Volume does not confirm.
- Breakout immediately closes back inside range.
- Spread/slippage is too high.
- Daily loss cap or prop rules are close to being hit.

7. Dashboard Output
Show:
- 9:30 Breakout Mode: ACTIVE / INACTIVE
- Opening Range High
- Opening Range Low
- Current Break Direction
- Breakout Quality Score
- Long Target Potential: 1:2 / 1:3 / 1:4 / 1:5
- Short Target Potential: 1:2 / 1:3 / 1:4 / 1:5
- Entry Status: WAIT / READY LONG / READY SHORT
- Reason for entry or rejection
- Suggested entry, stop, TP1, TP2, runner target
- Failure level
- Trade management instructions

Important:
This should be a professional breakout model, not a blind 9:30 entry. The bot should wait for confirmation, avoid chasing, and only take trades where the reward potential is clearly larger than the risk.
