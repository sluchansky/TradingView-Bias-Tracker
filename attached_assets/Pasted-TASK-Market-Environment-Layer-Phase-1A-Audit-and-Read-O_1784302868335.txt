TASK: Market Environment Layer — Phase 1A: Audit and Read-Only Shadow Mode

OBJECTIVE

Create the foundation for a Market Environment Layer that identifies:

* Overall market regime
* Sector rotation
* Dominant market theme
* Futures-market preferences
* Supporting and conflicting evidence

This phase must be read-only and shadow-mode only.

It must not:

* Change trade confidence
* Approve or reject trades
* Modify position sizing
* Pause entries
* Submit orders
* Change existing strategies
* Change existing backend order-routing logic
* Create a second news panel
* Add a new market-data provider
* Affect live trading in any way

The purpose of this phase is to prove that the bot can correctly observe and explain the market environment before allowing it to influence decisions.

Use existing indicators where available.

6. CREATE INITIAL REGIME RULES

Keep rules conservative and explainable.

Do not classify a regime from one input.

RISK_ON candidate:

Require at least three supporting observations such as:

* MNQ above VWAP
* MES above VWAP
* Technology outperforming broad market
* Consumer discretionary outperforming staples
* VIX falling
* Broad participation across sectors

RISK_OFF candidate:

Require at least three supporting observations such as:

* MNQ below VWAP
* MES below VWAP
* VIX rising
* Gold strengthening
* Defensive sectors outperforming
* Technology and consumer discretionary lagging

INFLATIONARY candidate:

Require multiple confirmations such as:

* Crude oil strengthening
* Energy sector outperforming
* Gold strengthening
* Rate-sensitive sectors weakening

GEOPOLITICAL candidate:

Require:

* Active relevant news from the existing news panel

And at least two market confirmations such as:

* Oil strength
* Gold strength
* Defense-sector strength
* VIX strength
* Equity weakness

News alone must never produce the GEOPOLITICAL regime.

MIXED:

Use when important signals conflict.

NEUTRAL:

Use when markets are balanced and data quality is sufficient.

UNKNOWN:

Use when data coverage or freshness is insufficient.

9. CALCULATE DATA QUALITY

Every snapshot must include a data-quality assessment.

Calculate:

* Expected input count
* Available input count
* Fresh input count
* Missing inputs
* Stale inputs
* Coverage percentage

Suggested classification:

90–100% fresh coverage:

HIGH data quality

70–89%:

MODERATE data quality

50–69%:

LOW data quality

Below 50%:

INSUFFICIENT data quality

When data quality is insufficient:

* Regime must be UNKNOWN
* Preferences must be INSUFFICIENT_DATA
* No market interpretation should be presented as reliable

Do not reuse the last known state without clearly marking it stale.
