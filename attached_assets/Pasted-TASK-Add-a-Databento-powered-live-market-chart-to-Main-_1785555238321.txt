Add a professional live price chart to Main Brain using the existing Databento market-data pipeline.

The chart should become the primary visual market view and show the same evidence used by the trading system.

This is a visualization and read-only data exposure task.

Do not create a second market-data feed.

Do not use TradingView as the chart data source.

PART 10 — UPDATE BEHAVIOR
==================================================

- stop on unmount
