3. PURPOSE OF INTRADAY_TREND

INTRADAY_TREND exists to capture substantial directional movement occurring during the current trading session.

Expected holding period:

approximately 15 minutes to several hours.

It is NOT:

* a scalp with a bigger target
* a swing trade closed early
* a fixed 1:1 system
* an Opening Range Breakout replacement

The dedicated 9:30 ORB algorithm remains separate.

INTRADAY_TREND should seek quality intraday structure and allow winning trades enough room to capture meaningful session movement.

8. THREE INTRADAY SETUP FAMILIES

Implement/retain exactly three primary setup families.

Do not add more strategies during this task.

A. TREND_PULLBACK

Purpose:

Join an established intraday directional move after a controlled retracement.

LONG example:

1. intraday trend bullish
2. bullish structure exists
3. price retraces toward meaningful support
4. support can include VWAP, FVG, OB, prior breakout level or recent structure
5. pullback does not invalidate trend
6. bullish rejection/reclaim occurs
7. sufficient room exists to next objective

SHORT = inverse.

Prefer this setup when the market is already trending.

⸻

B. LIQUIDITY_SWEEP_REVERSAL

Purpose:

Trade rejection after liquidity is taken from an important intraday level.

LONG example:

1. price sweeps a meaningful low
2. candidate levels include:
    * overnight low
    * London low
    * prior-day low
    * session low
    * significant recent swing low
3. price rejects/reclaims the level
4. structure confirms reversal/reclaim
5. stop can be placed beyond the sweep extreme
6. sufficient room exists to the next objective

SHORT = inverse.

Do NOT trade every wick as a sweep.

The swept level must be structurally meaningful.

⸻

C. BREAKOUT_RETEST

Purpose:

Join continuation after a meaningful intraday structure level breaks and successfully retests.

LONG example:

1. meaningful resistance breaks
2. price establishes acceptance above it
3. pullback/retest occurs
4. broken resistance acts as support
5. continuation confirmation appears
6. sufficient room exists to the next liquidity/structure target

SHORT = inverse.

This is separate from the dedicated 9:30 ORB algorithm.

12. STRUCTURAL STOP

INTRADAY_TREND must use a structural stop.

Do NOT use SWING’s ATR stop as the primary stop model.

Depending upon setup:

TREND_PULLBACK

Stop behind the structure whose failure invalidates the pullback thesis.

Possible examples:

* pullback swing
* OB invalidation
* FVG invalidation
* VWAP reclaim structure

LIQUIDITY_SWEEP_REVERSAL

Stop beyond the sweep extreme plus appropriate existing tick/volatility buffer.

BREAKOUT_RETEST

Stop beyond the failed retest/invalidation structure.

ATR may be used as a sanity check.

For example, reject obviously microscopic or absurdly large structural stops.

ATR should NOT simply become:

stop = entry ± X * ATR

for every intraday trade.

16. DO NOT MOVE TO BREAKEVEN TOO EARLY

This is important.

The bot historically has had trades reach TP1 and then get stopped at breakeven before the larger move.

INTRADAY_TREND should NOT automatically move the remaining position to exact breakeven merely because TP1 touched.

Prefer protection after confirmation such as:

* favorable BOS
* new protected swing
* strong continuation
* approximately 1.25R–1.5R excursion

Use existing management infrastructure where possible.

Do not create a second independent position manager.

The objective is to allow an intraday winner to breathe.

17. TRAILING

After the trade establishes a meaningful trend, trail structurally.

For LONG:

trail beneath confirmed higher lows/protected structure.

For SHORT:

trail above confirmed lower highs/protected structure.

Avoid tick-by-tick trailing.

Avoid excessively tight ATR trailing.

The objective is to capture the session move.
