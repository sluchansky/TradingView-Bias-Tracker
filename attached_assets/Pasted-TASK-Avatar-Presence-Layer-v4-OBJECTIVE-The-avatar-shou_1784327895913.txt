TASK: Avatar Presence Layer v4

OBJECTIVE

The avatar should feel alive.

Do NOT change:

- Trading logic
- Confidence
- Scoring
- Execution
- TradersPost
- Backend
- APIs
- Database

This is strictly a presentation and interaction layer.

--------------------------------------------------
1. EXPRESSIONS
--------------------------------------------------

Give the avatar emotional expressions based on market state.

Examples:

WAITING
- Relaxed posture
- Slow blinking
- Looking at charts

OBSERVING
- Eyes scanning
- Slight head movement

INTERESTED
- Looks toward user
- Raises eyebrows slightly

READY
- Makes eye contact
- Focused expression

TRADE ACTIVE
- Concentrated posture

WIN
- Small smile
- Subtle nod

LOSS
- Calm acknowledgement
- No exaggerated emotion

Never become cartoonish.

--------------------------------------------------
2. EYE CONTACT
--------------------------------------------------

The avatar should occasionally look at:

• The chart
• Dashboard widgets
• The user

During speech, primarily maintain eye contact.

When thinking, glance naturally toward market information.

--------------------------------------------------
3. IDLE ANIMATIONS
--------------------------------------------------

Never freeze.

Include subtle:

- breathing
- blinking
- posture shifts
- shoulder movement
- head adjustments

Animations should be slow and natural.

--------------------------------------------------
4. SPEAKING ANIMATION
--------------------------------------------------

Synchronize:

- mouth
- jaw
- cheeks
- subtle head movement

Speech should never look robotic.

--------------------------------------------------
5. ATTENTION SYSTEM
--------------------------------------------------

When something important happens:

READY
ENTRY
EXIT
TARGET
STOP

The avatar should immediately shift attention before speaking.

Example:

Looks at chart.

Turns toward user.

Begins speaking.

--------------------------------------------------
6. LISTENING MODE
--------------------------------------------------

When the microphone is active:

Maintain eye contact.

Occasionally nod.

Show subtle listening animation.

Stop all ambient narration.

--------------------------------------------------
7. THINKING MODE
--------------------------------------------------

When processing:

Look toward chart.

Small pause.

Return to user before speaking.

Never stare blankly.

--------------------------------------------------
8. MARKET REACTION
--------------------------------------------------

Different market states should produce different body language.

Quiet market

- relaxed

Fast market

- focused

High-confidence setup

- attentive

Trade management

- concentrated

Never overreact.

--------------------------------------------------
9. PERSONAL SPACE
--------------------------------------------------

Avoid constant movement.

Movement should be purposeful.

Small motions are preferable to large animations.

--------------------------------------------------
10. POLISH
--------------------------------------------------

Transitions between animations should be smooth.

No snapping.

No jitter.

No repeated looping every few seconds.

Everything should feel natural.

--------------------------------------------------
SUCCESS CRITERIA
--------------------------------------------------

The avatar should no longer feel like a talking dashboard.

It should feel like a calm professional trader sitting beside the user, watching the market, reacting naturally, maintaining eye contact, and communicating through subtle expressions and body language while the underlying trading engine remains completely unchanged.
