Upgrade the Session Story panel from a simple event log into a true AI Market Memory / Analyst Notebook.

Right now the panel lists events like:
- Edge jumped to 73
- Price lost VWAP
- Market phase: Trend Exhausted
- Edge dropped to 40

That is useful, but I want it to explain what those events mean and how they affect the current trade thesis.

Build the Session Story as a professional analyst-style summary with these sections:

1. Current Bias
Show:
- Bullish / Bearish / Neutral
- Probability estimate
- Why the bias exists

Example:
Current Bias: Bearish 62%
Reason: Price lost VWAP, trend is mature/exhausted, rallies are failing, sellers are still in short-term control.

2. Market Character
Show the current market state:
- Consolidation
- Pullback
- Trend Mature
- Trend Exhausted
- Breakout Attempt
- Failed Breakout
- Liquidity Sweep
- Mean Reversion
- Continuation

Do not just log the phase. Explain what it means.

Example:
Market Character: Trend Exhausted
Meaning: The move has already traveled far enough that chasing short entries is lower quality. Wait for a clean pullback, liquidity sweep, or fresh CHOCH before entering.

3. Current Thesis
Create a plain-English thesis.

Example:
The market is leaning short, but the short is not clean enough yet. Sellers are in control, but price is extended. The bot should wait for a liquidity sweep, failed VWAP reclaim, or CHOCH before taking action.

4. Most Likely Next Move
Show probabilities:
- Bearish continuation %
- Range / chop %
- Bullish reversal %

Example:
Most Likely Next:
Bearish continuation: 60%
Range/chop: 28%
Bullish reversal: 12%

5. Key Memory From Today
Instead of only logging events, summarize what the bot has learned today.

Examples:
- Every VWAP reclaim has failed so far.
- Long continuation attempts have been weak.
- Sellers controlled NY open.
- The best shorts came after failed rallies, not after chasing lows.
- Liquidity sweeps are producing better setups than breakout entries.

6. Trade Instructions
At the bottom, show exactly what the bot is waiting for.

Example:
Decision: WAIT

Do not short weakness.

Wait for:
- Liquidity sweep above a prior high
- Failed VWAP reclaim
- Bearish CHOCH
- Delta weakening
- Clean stop location

Invalid if:
- Price closes above VWAP
- Delta expands bullish
- Structure flips higher high / higher low

7. Event Timeline
Keep the timeline, but make each event explain impact.

Instead of:
Edge jumped to 73

Say:
08:47 - Edge jumped to 73 because price reclaimed VWAP and momentum improved. This briefly supported a long thesis.

Instead of:
Edge dropped to 40

Say:
09:18 - Edge dropped to 40 because VWAP reclaim failed and momentum weakened. Long thesis is no longer trusted.

8. Bot Self-Reflection
Add a short “Analyst Note” line.

Example:
Analyst Note:
I am leaning short, but I do not want to chase a mature move. I need either a pullback into resistance or a fresh liquidity sweep before this becomes tradeable.

Important:
This should not add new trade entries by itself. It should only improve explanation, memory, and decision clarity.

Goal:
Make Session Story feel like an analyst sitting next to me explaining:
- What happened
- What it means
- What the bot learned
- What it is waiting for
- What would invalidate the idea
