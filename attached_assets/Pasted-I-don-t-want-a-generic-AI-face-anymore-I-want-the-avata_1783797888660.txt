I don't want a generic AI face anymore.

I want the avatar to become the identity of the AI Trading Partner.

Think less "robot."

Think more "living digital intelligence."

Overall style
Minimal like OpenAI Voice Mode.
Premium like Apple Vision Pro.
Futuristic like Starlink or Neuralink.
Calm instead of flashy.
Dark background with subtle animated particles.
The avatar should feel alive even when it's silent.
Avatar design

The face should not look like a human.

It should be made from thousands of tiny moving particles, dots, and flowing light.

The particles should constantly reorganize themselves as if the AI is thinking.

No hard edges.

No stock-photo human.

No cheesy sci-fi robot.

The face should appear to exist inside a cloud of floating data.

Almost holographic.

Almost transparent.

Idle animation

When nothing is happening:

slow breathing
subtle head movement
particles drifting
eyes softly glowing
occasional blink
tiny ripple effects through the face

The AI should never look frozen.

Thinking animation

When analyzing:

The particles should become more active.

Small streams of light should travel across the face.

The eyes become slightly brighter.

A soft pulse radiates outward every few seconds.

The face subtly leans forward.

Speaking animation

When speaking:

The mouth should not lip-sync.

Instead:

particle movement increases
jaw area slightly reshapes
eyes animate
subtle facial expression changes
light pulses with speech

It should feel like an intelligence communicating—not an animated cartoon.

Listening mode

When I am talking:

The avatar focuses on me.

Eyes brighten.

Very slight head tilt.

Small ring of particles rotates around the head.

The status changes to:

Listening...

Background

The background should never be empty.

Very subtle animated particles.

Tiny moving stars.

Floating data.

Goal

I don't want users to feel like they're looking at a dashboard.

I want them to feel like they're sitting across from an intelligent trading analyst that is constantly watching the market and explaining its thoughts.

The avatar should become the personality of the AI Trading Partner and the central focus of the entire application.
