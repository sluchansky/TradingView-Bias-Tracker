=========================
MISSION
=========================

The AI Trading Partner is no longer just a trading bot.

The vision is to become an intelligent trading workspace that combines:

• Trading Desktop
• Trading Expert
• Trading Partner
• Trading Coach
• Trading Journal
• Trading Manager

into one unified dashboard.

Before any additional development, we need a complete inventory of what already exists.

Do NOT recommend code changes.
