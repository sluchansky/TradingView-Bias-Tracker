Core idea:

Micro Scalp Mode logic:

1. Liquidity Sweep Detection
    Look for:

* price sweeping prior high or prior low
* stop run above/below recent range
* fast candle expansion
* volume spike
* delta/CVD imbalance if available
* wick rejection after sweep

2. Trap Detection
    Bot should ask:

* who is trapped?
* did longs get trapped above highs?
* did shorts get trapped below lows?
* did price fail to continue after the sweep?

3. Absorption Detection
    Look for:

* strong buying/selling pressure but price stops moving
* delta disagrees with price
* high volume but limited price progress
* wick rejection
* failed breakout or failed breakdown

Use available:

* seconds data if available
* 15 second / 1 minute logic
* micro CHOCH

* candle close back inside range after sweep
