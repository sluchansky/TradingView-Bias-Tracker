MNQ/MGC Performance Audit & Improvements

Current Stats:

* Total Setups: 50
* Trades Taken: 46
* Win Rate: 48%
* Net P&L: +$148
* Wins: 22
* Losses: 24
* Best Instrument: MNQ (+$113)
* Worst Instrument: MGC (+$35)
* Strong Trades: 0

* Average Edge Score: 55
* Highest Edge Score: 60

Observations:

1. The bot is profitable despite a sub-50% win rate, meaning winners are larger than losers.
2. The bot is taking 46 out of 50 possible setups (92%), which indicates it is too permissive and entering too many average-quality trades.
3. No setups have been classified as Strong Trades or A+ Setups.
4. The highest edge score is only 60, suggesting the scoring system may be capped incorrectly or confirmations are not adding points properly.

Requested Changes:

1. Reclassify setup tiers:

* Possible Trade: Edge Score >= 50
* Strong Trade: Edge Score >= 70
* A+ Setup: Edge Score >= 85

2. Implement weighted scoring:

BOS Confirmed = +20 points
CHOCH Confirmed = +20 points
VWAP Reclaim/Rejection = +15 points
Liquidity Sweep = +15 points
Volume Spike or RVOL Confirmation = +15 points
CVD Agreement = +15 points
Session Bonus (5:00 AM–8:00 AM ET, 8:00 AM–11:00 AM ET, 8:00 PM–11:00 PM ET) = +10 points

3. Tighten READY alerts:

Only send READY LONG or READY SHORT alerts if:

* Edge Score >= 70
* Volume confirmed
* Market structure confirmed
* Price is near VWAP or an active supply/demand zone

4. Add diagnostics to every setup:

Display:

* Final Edge Score
* Points earned from each confirmation
* Which confirmations failed
* Why the setup did or did not become READY
* Maximum possible score
* Whether any score cap was applied

5. Audit scoring engine:

Investigate why no trade has scored above 60.
Determine:

* Are confirmations not adding points correctly?
* Is there an unintended score cap?
* Are points being overwritten instead of accumulated?
* Are filters blocking higher scores from being achieved?

Goal:
Reduce low-quality trades, increase selectivity, produce fewer but higher-quality setups, and allow the bot to generate Strong Trades and A+ setups with significantly higher confidence and consistency.
