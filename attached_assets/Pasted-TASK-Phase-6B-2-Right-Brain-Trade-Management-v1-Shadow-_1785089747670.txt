TASK: Phase 6B.2 — Right Brain Trade Management v1 (Shadow Mode)

OBJECTIVE

Build Trade Management Brain v1 as a dedicated subsystem INSIDE the existing
Right Brain.

The Right Brain is responsible for:

- active trade awareness
- trade health
- thesis monitoring
- risk warnings
- management recommendations
- post-entry decision support

The Left Brain must remain responsible for:

- market scanning
- opportunity discovery
- broad analysis
- strategy context
- candidate setup evaluation

THIS PHASE IS SHADOW MODE ONLY.

Do not modify live exits.

Do not modify stops.

Do not modify targets.

Do not modify TradersPost.

Do not modify Databento.

Do not modify strategy detectors.

Do not modify entry scoring.

Do not modify learning.

====================================================
PART 1 — ARCHITECTURE
====================================================

Preserve the Left Brain / Right Brain separation.

Required architecture:

Databento
    │
    ├──────────────► Left Brain
    │                  - scans markets
    │                  - finds opportunities
    │                  - analyzes conditions
    │                  - identifies candidate strategies
    │
    ▼
Strategy Engine
    │
    ▼
Trade Plan
    │
    ▼
Execution
    │
    ▼
Right Brain
    ├── Active Trade State
    ├── Trade Management Brain v1
    ├── Thesis Monitor
    ├── Risk Monitor
    └── Shadow Management Recommendation
             │
             ▼
        Dashboard / Decision Center

The Trade Management Brain must be implemented as part of the Right Brain,
not as a separate top-level brain.

====================================================
PART 2 — RIGHT BRAIN OWNERSHIP
====================================================

The Right Brain should have two clear operating states:

STATE 1 — FLAT

When no active trade exists:

- observe candidate trade plans
- display readiness
- show "No Active Trade"
- do not generate post-entry management recommendations
- do not duplicate Left Brain analysis

STATE 2 — ACTIVE TRADE

When an active trade exists:

- monitor the trade continuously
- compare current market state with the original thesis
- calculate trade health
- calculate thesis drift
- calculate exit pressure
- issue shadow management recommendations
- never change execution during this phase

====================================================
PART 3 — RIGHT BRAIN MODULE BOUNDARY
====================================================

Create or extend a clearly named Right Brain function such as:

compute_right_brain_trade_management(...)

or:

compute_trade_management_brain(...)

The function must live next to the existing Right Brain logic where practical.

Do not place the primary management logic inside:

- Left Brain functions

- TradersPost gateway
- learning engine
- dashboard JavaScript

Dashboard code may only render the returned result.

Preferred output attachment:

If the current Right Brain result uses another established schema, preserve it
and add the management block inside that structure.

Do not create a competing top-level:

result["trade_management"]

unless the existing architecture makes nesting impossible.

====================================================

Use existing stored trade metadata when available:

The module must continue safely.

====================================================
PART 6 — MANAGEMENT ANALYSIS
====================================================

During an active trade, the Right Brain evaluates:

MOMENTUM

- increasing
- stable
- weakening
- reversing
- unavailable

TREND

- strengthening
- intact
- weakening
- broken
- unavailable

VWAP

- holding in trade direction
- neutral
- failing
- reclaimed
- lost
- unavailable

STRUCTURE

- intact
- threatened
- broken
- favorable break
- unavailable

VOLUME

- expanding
- normal
- contracting
- exhaustion
- unavailable

DELTA / CVD

- confirming
- neutral
- diverging
- reversing
- unavailable

VOLATILITY

- expanding favorably
- stable
- contracting
- expanding adversely
- unavailable

TRADE PROGRESS

- not progressing
- progressing normally
- progressing strongly
- stalled
- near stop
- near target
- unavailable

None of these evaluations may modify execution.

====================================================
PART 7 — TRADE HEALTH SCORE
====================================================

Create:

trade_health_score: 0–100

trade_health_label:

- EXCELLENT
- STRONG
- HEALTHY
- NEUTRAL
- WEAK
- DANGER
- CRITICAL

This score describes the health of an already-open trade.

It is not:

- edge score
- setup confidence
- eligibility confidence
- learning score
- entry confidence

Return:

- score
- label
- reasons
- warnings
- supporting signals
- conflicting signals

Document the weights used.

Cap the score between 0 and 100.

Missing inputs must reduce certainty, not automatically mark the trade bad.

====================================================
PART 8 — MANAGEMENT CONFIDENCE
====================================================

Create:

management_confidence: 0–100

management_confidence_label:

- HIGH
- MEDIUM
- LOW
- INSUFFICIENT_DATA

This measures confidence in the management diagnosis, not confidence in the
trade itself.

Examples:

- Strong agreement across trend, momentum, structure, and VWAP:
  high management confidence

- Most inputs unavailable:
  insufficient data

Do not confuse trade health with diagnostic confidence.

====================================================
PART 9 — THESIS DRIFT
====================================================

Return:

thesis_status:

- IMPROVING
- INTACT
- STABLE
- WEAKENING
- BROKEN
- UNKNOWN

Compare current conditions with the entry-time thesis.

Return:

- original thesis factors
- current thesis factors
- factors still valid
- factors weakened
- factors invalidated
- explanation

A thesis may be weakening while the trade is profitable.

A thesis may remain intact while the trade is temporarily negative.

Do not use P&L alone to classify thesis status.

====================================================
PART 10 — EXIT PRESSURE
====================================================

Return:

exit_pressure:

- NONE
- LOW
- MODERATE
- ELEVATED
- HIGH
- CRITICAL

Exit pressure should consider:

- stop proximity
- structure break
- VWAP failure
- momentum reversal
- adverse delta
- volatility expansion against the trade
- time decay / trade stagnation
- opposing zone proximity
- target proximity
- current R
- original invalidation condition

This remains diagnostic only.

Do not send an exit.

Do not move a stop.

Do not reduce quantity.

====================================================
PART 12 — DIRECTION-AWARE LOGIC
====================================================

All management logic must correctly invert for Long and Short trades.

Examples:

Long:

- price above VWAP may be favorable
- higher highs may support thesis
- downside structure break may weaken thesis

Short:

- price below VWAP may be favorable
- lower lows may support thesis
- upside structure break may weaken thesis

Add mirrored tests proving Long and Short logic behaves correctly.

Do not assume every active trade is Long.

====================================================
PART 13 — TIME-IN-TRADE LOGIC
====================================================

Use mode-aware expectations.

SCALP:

- short time horizon
- stagnation becomes relevant sooner
- near-term momentum matters more

SWING:

- longer time horizon
- normal pullbacks should not automatically create high exit pressure
- higher-timeframe structure matters more

Do not change the existing mode definitions.

Do not hard-code one time threshold for all strategies and modes.

Where reliable strategy-specific timing is unavailable, use conservative
diagnostic defaults and label them as assumptions.

====================================================
PART 14 — STRATEGY-AWARE MANAGEMENT
====================================================

The Right Brain may interpret active-trade health differently by strategy.

Examples:

OPENING_DRIVE

- early momentum and continuation are important
- immediate failure back into the opening range is concerning

OPENING_RANGE_BREAKOUT

- holding outside the range supports thesis
- returning into the range weakens thesis

LIQUIDITY_SWEEP_REVERSAL

- reclaim and follow-through support thesis
- returning through the sweep extreme weakens thesis

VWAP_TREND_CONTINUATION

- holding the correct side of VWAP supports thesis
- loss of VWAP and structure weakens thesis

RANGE_EXPANSION_BREAKOUT

- range acceptance supports thesis
- failed breakout and re-entry into balance weaken thesis

Do not alter detectors or entry criteria.

These are post-entry diagnostic interpretations only.

}
