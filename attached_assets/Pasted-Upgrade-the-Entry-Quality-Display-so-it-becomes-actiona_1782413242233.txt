Upgrade the Entry Quality Display so it becomes actionable, not just diagnostic.

Current issue:
The bot correctly identifies poor trade location, but it only says “Poor Location” or “Overextended.” I need it to also explain where a better entry would be and what conditions would improve the score.

Changes needed:

1. Add “Better Entry Zone”
When Entry Quality is below 70, show a better entry area.

Use:
- VWAP pullback zone
- nearest demand/supply zone
- 50% pullback of current impulse
- prior breakout/retest level

Example:
Better Entry Zone: 4037.50 - 4039.00
Reason: closer to VWAP and demand support.

2. Add “What Improves This Trade”
Show exactly what would raise the score.

Example:
Score now: 62/100

Would improve if:
- Price pulls back closer to VWAP: +12
- Bullish candle confirms support: +8
- CVD/order flow confirms buyers: +10
- Liquidity sweep occurs first: +6

Projected score if confirmed: 88/100

3. Change ATR Extension scoring
Right now ATR Extension goes straight to 0 when price is too extended. Make it graduated:

0-1 ATR from VWAP = 12 points
1-2 ATR = 10 points
2-3 ATR = 7 points
3-4 ATR = 4 points
4-5 ATR = 2 points
Over 5 ATR = 0 points

4. Add “Chasing Warning”
If price is more than 3 ATR from VWAP, display:

CHASING WARNING:
Price is extended from fair value. Do not enter unless there is a fresh pullback/retest or strong order-flow confirmation.

5. Add “Entry Plan”
Instead of only saying WAIT, show the next actionable step.

Example:
ENTRY PLAN:
Wait for pullback into 4037.50 - 4039.00.
Then require:
- bullish rejection candle
- CVD/Delta confirmation
- stop below demand
- target back toward recent high

6. Keep Entry Quality separate from Edge Score
A trade can have good direction but bad location.
Do not let Entry Quality fully replace the main Edge Score.
Use Entry Quality as a final execution filter.

Rules:
- Edge Score = setup quality
- Entry Quality = whether the current price is a smart entry
- If Edge is strong but Entry Quality is poor, verdict should be WAIT FOR BETTER ENTRY, not NO TRADE.
