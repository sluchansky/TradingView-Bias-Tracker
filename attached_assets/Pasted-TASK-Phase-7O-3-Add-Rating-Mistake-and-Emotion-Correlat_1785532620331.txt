TASK: Phase 7O.3 — Add Rating × Mistake and Emotion Correlation Analytics to Journal Coaching.

CONTEXT

Journal Coaching now includes:

- costliest mistakes
- best behaviors
- followed-plan analysis
- emotions
- quality ratings
- strategy/session coaching
- drill-down evidence
- 30-minute intraday analysis

The next goal is to determine how operator ratings relate to repeated mistakes, emotions, and trade outcomes.

PRIMARY QUESTIONS

The system should answer:

- Which mistakes most often occur on low-discipline trades?
- Which emotions overlap with poor execution?
- Are high-quality setups being ruined by poor execution?
- Do low setup ratings predict poor results?
- Are losing trades still high-quality when the plan was followed?
- Which behavioral combinations are most expensive?

This is read-only analytics and display.

STRICT SCOPE

DO NOT modify:

- journal trade truth
- review data
- coaching formulas already in production
- learning formulas
- learning thresholds
- strategy scoring
- readiness
- risk
- alerts
- TradersPost
- execution
- Databento
- Main Brain polling
- authentication

PART 1 — DEFINE ANALYSIS POPULATION

Use only closed reviewed trades with valid review ratings.

Track coverage separately for:

- total reviewed trades
- trades with setup rating
- trades with execution rating
- trades with discipline rating
- trades with overall rating
- trades with mistake tags
- trades with emotion tags
- trades excluded from analysis

Do not treat missing ratings as zero.

PART 3 — RATING × MISTAKE MATRIX

For every rating dimension and mistake tag, calculate:

- occurrence count
- percentage of trades in that rating band
- win rate
- net P&L
- net R
- average R
- followed-plan rate
- confidence state

Example:

DISCIPLINE LOW × CHASED ENTRY
9 trades
64% of low-discipline trades
-4.8R
22% followed-plan rate

Do not imply causation.

Label results as associations.

- average R

- followed-plan rate

PART 7 — HIGH-QUALITY LOSSES

- setup quality 4–5
- execution quality 4–5

- count
- average R

- instruments
- screenshots/reviews available

PART 8 — LOW-QUALITY WINS

Identify trades meeting one or more of:

- discipline quality 1–2
- execution quality 1–2

- realized R > 0

Label:

LOW-QUALITY WIN

Show:

- count
- net R
- most common mistake
- most common emotion
- strategy/session concentration

Explain clearly:

Profitable outcome does not validate poor process.
