TASK: Final Production Validation — Decision Pipeline V2

OBJECTIVE

Before enabling Decision Pipeline V2 in production, prove beyond reasonable doubt that it does NOT alter live trading behavior.

This is a validation task ONLY.

DO NOT modify trading logic.

DO NOT modify scoring.

DO NOT modify broker routing.

DO NOT modify TradersPost.

DO NOT modify Discord.

DO NOT modify production execution.

Legacy Production Pipeline

Decision Pipeline V2

at the same time.

• Broker Payload
• TradersPost Payload
• Discord Payload

• Routing Decisions

• Technical Verdict
• Blocking Factors

narrative_summary

Nothing else may change.
