=========================================================
PHASE 4 FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS
=========================================================

Implement and validate the ten roadmap tasks exactly as documented.

The following explanation states must be represented using authoritative runtime data.

---------------------------------------------------------
A. MARKET_CLOSED
---------------------------------------------------------

When the market or instrument session is closed, the operator explanation must make that state understandable.

It must distinguish:

- session closed
- market-data unavailable
- maintenance or halt state
- ordinary WAIT conditions

Do not report a market as closed merely because data is stale.

Use the existing CME/session owner.

Do not add a new competing session calculation.

---------------------------------------------------------
B. WAIT
---------------------------------------------------------

WAIT explanations must communicate the actual failed conditions.

Use the canonical failed-gate evidence already produced by the decision pipeline.

Do not recompute gate truth independently.

Do not turn a generic absence of readiness into a fabricated failure reason.

Preserve:

- current verdict
- confidence
- edge score
- actionability
- strategy result
- direction

---------------------------------------------------------
C. VETO_ACTIVE
---------------------------------------------------------

When a veto blocks an otherwise meaningful setup, the explanation must identify:

- that a veto is active
- the authoritative veto source
- the blocking reason when available
- the distinction between veto and ordinary failed confirmation

Do not convert every failed condition into a veto.

Do not change veto logic.

---------------------------------------------------------
D. READY
---------------------------------------------------------

READY explanations must communicate why the setup is actionable using existing decision evidence.

Do not manufacture certainty.

Do not change the readiness threshold.

Do not change confidence or edge score.

---------------------------------------------------------
E. EARLY
---------------------------------------------------------

EARLY must remain distinct from READY and WAIT.

The explanation must communicate that a potential setup exists but required confirmation or timing is incomplete.

Use the authoritative potential-plan and direction data discovered in Stage 2.

Do not introduce a new trade state merely for UI convenience.

---------------------------------------------------------
F. ACTIVE TRADE
---------------------------------------------------------

When a trade is active, the operator explanation must prioritize active-trade management over a new-entry narrative.

Use the canonical Manager or active-trade owner.

Do not recompute stop, target, risk, or trade state.

Do not expose mutable internal objects.

---------------------------------------------------------
G. THESIS_INVALIDATED
---------------------------------------------------------

When a true thesis-invalidating event exists, explain:

- that the prior thesis was invalidated
- the authoritative invalidating event or status
- the reason when available
- timing when available
- current thesis state

Do not fabricate invalidation from missing data.

Do not equate `CONFLICTED`, `WAIT`, or `OUTLOOK_SHIFT` with invalidation unless the architecture explicitly defines that mapping.

---------------------------------------------------------
H. PARTNER FALLBACK
---------------------------------------------------------

If Partner explanation generation fails, the surrounding status or decision surface must remain available.

Requirements:

- fail safely
- preserve the underlying decision result
- provide a deterministic fallback explanation
- do not return HTTP 500 solely because the Partner explanation failed
- do not hide the failure completely
- do not place orders
- do not write unexpected database state

This is fault-containment work only.

Do not broaden it into a Partner refactor.
