1. TRADE MANAGEMENT ENGINE

When a READY trade is detected, generate a full trade management plan.

Include:

* Entry
* Stop
* TP1
* TP2
* TP3
* Move Stop to Breakeven level
* Partial profit level
* Runner target
* Risk in points
* Reward-to-risk estimate
* Max invalidation condition

Example:

LONG READY

Entry: 2958.4
Stop: 2955.8

TP1: 2960.0
TP2: 2962.5
TP3: 2966.0

Move Stop to BE: 2960.2
Partial: 2961.5
Runner Target: 2966.0

Risk: 2.6 points
Reward: 7.6 points
R:R: 2.9R

3. TRADE OUTCOME TRACKING

When a trade closes, generate a trade outcome message.

Track:

* Result: Win, Loss, or Breakeven
* R Multiple
* Entry
* Stop
* Final Exit
* Max favorable move
* Max adverse move
* Setup type
* Trade strength: Possible, Strong, or A+
* Edge Score
* Instrument
* Direction
* Reason trade closed

Example:

Trade Closed

Result: WIN
R Multiple: +3.2R

Setup:
Liquidity Sweep + Zone Mitigation + VWAP Reclaim + Bullish CHOCH

Instrument: MGC
Direction: LONG
Edge Score: 94
Trade Strength: Strong

Reason:
TP2 reached after VWAP reclaim continuation.

5. A+ SETUP ROUTING

Add an A+ setup category.

Classification:

* Possible Trade: Edge Score 75–89
* Strong Trade: Edge Score 90–94
* A+ Setup: Edge Score 95–100

For A+ setups, add a stronger Discord label:

🔥 A+ SETUP

If Discord channel routing is already supported, allow A+ setups to be sent to a separate channel.

If separate channel routing is not configured, keep the alert in the existing channel and simply label it clearly.

Do not block Possible or Strong alerts.

6. EXPLAIN WHY COMMAND

Add a command or endpoint that explains why the latest trade is READY.

Command examples:

/why
/why MGC
/why MNQ

Response should include:

* Direction
* Edge Score
* Trade Strength
* Required READY conditions that passed
* Confluences present
* Risk factors
* What would invalidate the setup
* What would improve the setup

Example:

Why is MGC LONG READY?

READY Gate Passed:
✓ BOS Demand
✓ Bullish CHOCH
✓ Confirmation candle
✓ VWAP Reclaim
✓ Active timing window
✓ No opposing full setup

Confluences:
✓ Liquidity Sweep
✓ Demand Zone Mitigated
✓ Price near demand zone

Risk:
⚠ Nearby resistance

Invalidation:
Close below demand zone or VWAP failure.

Would improve if:
Price holds above VWAP and TP1 is reached.
