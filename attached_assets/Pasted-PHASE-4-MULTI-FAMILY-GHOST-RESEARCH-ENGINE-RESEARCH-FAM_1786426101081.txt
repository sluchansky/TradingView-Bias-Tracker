5. FVG CREATION IS NOT AN OPPORTUNITY
==================================================

An FVG merely existing is NOT enough to create a Ghost opportunity.

Distinguish:

FVG_CREATED

from:

FVG_REVISIT_OPPORTUNITY

The research opportunity occurs when price subsequently interacts with a valid active FVG in a manner that could plausibly produce a trade.

Example:

bullish FVG created
    ↓
market moves away
    ↓
price later revisits FVG
    ↓
FVG_REVISIT opportunity created

Do not create an experiment every bar while price remains inside the same FVG.
