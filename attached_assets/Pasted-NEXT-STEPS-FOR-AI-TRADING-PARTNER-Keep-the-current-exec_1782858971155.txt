NEXT STEPS FOR AI TRADING PARTNER

Keep the current execution/risk architecture. Do NOT remove safety gates, daily loss cap, prop-firm guard, max contracts, cooldowns, broker armed requirement, or audit logs.

The next upgrade is not more indicators. The next upgrade is teaching the bot to understand market state before selecting a trade.

1. Add a Market State Engine

Before scoring any strategy, classify the current instrument into one of these states:

- TREND_UP
- TREND_DOWN
- PULLBACK
- RANGE
- BREAKOUT
- REVERSAL
- EXPANSION
- COMPRESSION
- LIQUIDITY_HUNT

This should be shown on the dashboard and included in Discord/output logs.

Example:
Market State: TREND_DOWN
Allowed strategies:
- VWAP Reject Short
- Pullback Short
- Liquidity Sweep Short

Blocked strategies:
- Trend Long
- Breakout Long
- Random Reversal Long

2. Replace single-alert direction with Directional Confidence

Do not let only BOS/CHOCH alerts decide direction.

Create a Directional Confidence Score from 0–100 for long and short separately.

Inputs should include:

- BOS / CHOCH
- VWAP position
- EMA alignment
- CVD / delta trend
- higher timeframe trend
- swing structure
- volume / RVOL
- liquidity sweep direction
- recent price action momentum

Example:
Long Confidence: 32
Short Confidence: 78
Directional Bias: SHORT

If BOS/CHOCH is missing but every other major factor is bearish, the bot should still be able to consider shorts instead of being pinned long.

3. Add Trend Memory

The bot should remember the active market bias instead of fully resetting on every alert.

Track:

- currentTrend
- trendConfidence
- trendStartedAt
- lastConfirmedStructure
- trendStrength
- trendWeakening
- invalidationLevel

Example:
Current Trend: BEARISH
Confidence: 84%
Started: 9:42 AM
Trend Status: Strengthening

Only flip trend when opposite confidence is strong enough, not from one random candle.

4. Add Higher Timeframe Weighting

For scalping, use multi-timeframe context.

Suggested weighting:

- 15m trend/context = 30%
- 5m structure/setup = 40%
- 1m entry/timing = 30%

The bot should avoid taking 1m longs directly into a bearish 15m/5m trend unless it is specifically classified as a reversal with strong evidence.

5. Add Momentum / Velocity Analysis

Use second-based data and fast alerts to measure speed.

Track:

- price velocity
- candle expansion
- delta acceleration
- volume acceleration
- pullback speed
- rejection strength
- exhaustion signals
- first pullback after expansion

Goal:
Catch the move earlier instead of buying the last candle of the push.

6. Add Confidence Decay

Old setups should lose strength over time.

Example:
Edge Score starts at 92.
If no follow-through happens:

92 → 81 → 73 → 58 → WAIT

Decay should happen faster during scalping mode and slower during swing mode.

7. Add Adaptive Strategy Selection

The bot should not evaluate every strategy equally in every market.

First classify market state.
Then allow only strategies that fit that state.

Examples:

TREND_DOWN:
- allow VWAP reject short
- allow pullback short
- allow liquidity sweep short
- block weak long reversals

RANGE:
- allow range fade
- allow liquidity sweep reversal
- block trend continuation unless breakout confirmed

EXPANSION:
- allow continuation
- block countertrend entries

COMPRESSION:
- wait for breakout or sweep
- reduce size or avoid chop

8. Dashboard / Logs Required

Add these fields to dashboard and Discord output:

- Market State
- Long Confidence
- Short Confidence
- Directional Bias
- Trend Memory
- Higher Timeframe Alignment
- Momentum Status
- Confidence Decay
- Allowed Strategies
- Blocked Strategies
- Why trade was accepted or rejected

The bot should explain:

“What market are we in?”
“Which side has control?”
“Which strategies are allowed?”
“Why is this trade valid or blocked?”

9. Important Rule

Do not weaken risk management to make the bot trade more.

The goal is not more trades.
The goal is better trade selection and earlier entries.

Keep all safety gates fail-closed.
Only improve the intelligence before the READY gate.
