PART 3 — DEFINE BAR-CLOSE REPLAY SEMANTICS
----------------------------------------------------

The backtester must operate only on information available at the current
historical bar.

At replay index i:

- The detector may read candles[0:i+1]
- It must not read candles[i+1:] or any precomputed value containing future bars
- The opening range may use only bars whose timestamps are within the completed
  range window
- A breakout cannot trigger before the opening-range window is complete
- A breakout is recognized at bar close unless the existing backtest engine has
  an established and tested intrabar convention

Do not fabricate the order in which a bar touched:

- entry
- stop
- target
- opening-range boundary

Use the existing conservative same-bar conflict policy.

Document that policy before implementation.
