The dashboard is strong now, but I want the Main Brain to become the single final decision engine.

Right now multiple panels can disagree:
- Main Brain says building long case
- Structure says bearish
- Index alignment says bearish
- Strategy Radar shows long setups
- Live recommendation says WAIT

That is okay, but the dashboard must explain how those conflicts combine into one final decision.

Please build a “Main Brain Judge” layer at the top of the dashboard.

It should show:

1. FINAL DECISION
- READY LONG
- READY SHORT
- WAIT
- SKIP
- MANAGE OPEN TRADE

2. Decision hierarchy
Example:
Market structure: Bearish
Short-term order flow: Bullish
Strategy signals: 2 long setups
Risk filters: poor room to zone
Cross-market alignment: bearish
Final decision: WAIT

3. Weighted score breakdown
Show every factor and its point impact:
- Trend
- VWAP
- Demand/Supply zone
- FVG
- Order block
- CHOCH/BOS
- CVD/Delta
- RVOL/Volume
- Index alignment
- Room to opposing zone
- Risk/reward
- News filter
- Prop firm protection

Example:
VWAP +12
Demand +14
FVG +8
CHOCH 0
CVD -8
Index alignment -12
Room to zone -18
Total 55/100
Decision WAIT

4. Missing confirmations
Show exactly what is missing and how many points each item would add.

Example:
Missing:
CHOCH +25
CVD agreement +15
Liquidity sweep +10
Room to zone +20

Current score: 63
Ready threshold: 80
Needed: +17

5. Strategy trust scores
Each of the 16 strategies should have:
- Current signal: LONG / SHORT / NEUTRAL
- Confidence
- Historical win rate
- Average R
- Sample size
- Trust score
- Whether the Main Brain accepted or overruled it

Do not treat every strategy equally. Weight strategies higher if they have performed better in similar market conditions.

6. Decision quality tracking
Track whether WAIT, READY, SKIP, and MANAGE decisions were correct after price develops.

Example:
Decision: WAIT
Result: price reversed
Outcome: correct wait

Decision: WAIT
Result: price rallied 40 points
Outcome: missed opportunity
Lesson: CHOCH threshold may be too strict

7. Fix data consistency
All panels must use the same source of truth for:
- price
- VWAP
- structure
- CVD/delta
- index alignment
- active instrument
- current bias
- final verdict

If CVD is Unknown in one panel, no other panel should claim CVD confirmation unless it explains the fallback/proxy being used.

8. Fix object rendering bug
Learning Stats currently shows:
Best setup: [object Object]
Best window: [object Object]
Worst window: [object Object]

Render the actual fields instead:
- name
- win rate
- avg R
- sample size
- time window

Goal:
The Main Brain should not just list signals. It should act like a judge/investment committee that weighs all evidence, resolves conflicts, explains why it is waiting or ready, and shows exactly what would change the decision.
