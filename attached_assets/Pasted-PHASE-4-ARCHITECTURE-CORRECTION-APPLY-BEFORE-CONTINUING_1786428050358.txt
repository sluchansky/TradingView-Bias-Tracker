==================================================
1. DO NOT OVERLOAD "strategy" AS "strategy_family"
==================================================

The audit proposed:

ghost_opportunities.strategy doubles as family tag

DO NOT do that.

We are building a multi-family research platform and need explicit separation.

Add:

strategy_family

as a first-class field.

Required initial values:

09:30_ORB
FVG_REVISIT

Keep:

strategy

for the actual strategy/baseline identity.

Examples:

strategy_family = 09:30_ORB
strategy = CASH_OPEN_ORB_V2

or current exact ORB strategy identity.

FVG:

strategy_family = FVG_REVISIT
strategy = FVG_RESEARCH_BASELINE_V1

Do not make:

strategy = FVG_REVISIT

unless that genuinely is the strategy name.

4. DETERMINISTIC FVG RESEARCH ID
==================================================

The authoritative FVG engine currently uses:

uuid4()

That is acceptable for its runtime implementation, but Ghost Research must NOT rely on that random UUID as its sole replay identity.

Preserve the original engine ID as:

source_fvg_id

Then derive a stable research identity from immutable creation attributes.

Use a deterministic hash/versioned key based on appropriate fields such as:

instrument
direction
creation bar timestamp
upper boundary
lower boundary
FVG engine/version

Example conceptual field:

research_fvg_id

This same historical FVG replayed from the same Databento bars must receive the SAME research_fvg_id.

Do not include mutable fields such as:

touch_count
mitigation
current status

in the creation identity.

==================================================
5. REVISIT IDENTITY
==================================================

One FVG can have multiple legitimate revisits.

Use a separate deterministic:

revisit_id

Based on:

research_fvg_id
revisit number and/or canonical revisit bar timestamp
strategy version

Repeated processing of the same physical revisit must produce the same revisit_id.

A genuinely later revisit must receive a different revisit_id.

==================================================

source_fvg_id
research_fvg_id
revisit_id
