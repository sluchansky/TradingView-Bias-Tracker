1. Fix the VWAP session reset before we validate it

Replit says:

“Session VWAP resets at UTC hour 22 (6 PM ET)”

That is only 6 PM Eastern during daylight-saving time. In winter, 22:00 UTC = 5 PM ET.

The reset should be defined as 18:00 America/New_York, then converted appropriately. Otherwise VWAP will silently reset an hour early for part of the year.

That's important enough to fix before we start judging VWAP agreement.
