5. WHAT CREATES AN OPPORTUNITY
==================================================

Do NOT create an opportunity on every bar.

Do NOT create one for every WAIT state.

The opportunity boundary must come from a legitimate OrbEngine event.

Examples:

BREAKOUT_DETECTED
CONFIRMATION_PENDING
QUALIFIED

or the closest canonical event boundary appropriate to each experiment.

For the initial pilot, use one canonical pre-filter breakout opportunity event so rejected setups can still be researched.

A trade that had a legitimate breakout but failed:

trend
VWAP
CVD
VIX
confirmation
max-chase

may still be stored as a ghost opportunity.

A random bar inside the opening range must NOT create one.

Prevent duplicate opportunities from repeated polling of the same OrbEngine state.
