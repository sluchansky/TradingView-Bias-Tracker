STOP treating the avatar as a single drawing.

I want The Sentinel built as a layered rendering system.

Every facial feature should be an independent layer that can animate separately.

The avatar should be assembled from the following layers.

------------------------------------

LAYER 1

Background

- Gradient background
- Soft particles
- Floating stars
- Atmospheric fog

------------------------------------

LAYER 2

Energy Halo

Large glowing ring behind the head.

Animated.

Slow rotation.

Brightness changes with confidence.

------------------------------------

LAYER 3

Outer Energy Shell

Soft holographic glow.

Breathing animation.

Subtle transparency.

------------------------------------

LAYER 4

Head Shape

Smooth matte black mask.

No facial details.

No skin.

No textures.

Only the overall head silhouette.

------------------------------------

LAYER 5

Internal Lighting

Blue light moving beneath the surface.

Very subtle.

Should make the mask feel alive.

------------------------------------

LAYER 6

Eye Sockets

Soft shadow around the eyes.

Creates depth.

------------------------------------

LAYER 7

Eyebrows

Independent animation.

Move slightly while thinking.

------------------------------------

LAYER 8

Eyeballs

Independent.

Move together.

Track dashboard elements.

Maintain eye contact.

------------------------------------

LAYER 9

Irises

Glow independently.

Brightness changes.

Pupil dilation.

------------------------------------

LAYER 10

Eye Reflections

Multiple reflection layers.

Tiny moving highlights.

------------------------------------

LAYER 11

Upper Eyelids

Separate animation.

------------------------------------

LAYER 12

Lower Eyelids

Separate animation.

------------------------------------

LAYER 13

Blink Layer

Single animation controlling eyelids.

------------------------------------

LAYER 14

Forehead Energy

Soft moving energy.

Shows processing activity.

------------------------------------

LAYER 15

Mouth Line

Minimal line while idle.

------------------------------------

LAYER 16

Upper Lip

Independent.

------------------------------------

LAYER 17

Lower Lip

Independent.

------------------------------------

LAYER 18

Jaw

Moves slightly while speaking.

------------------------------------

LAYER 19

Speech Glow

Subtle pulse around the mouth while talking.

LAYER 20

Thinking Particles

------------------------------------

LAYER 21

Confidence Ring

Changes color.

Blue

Yellow

Orange

Green

Red

------------------------------------

LAYER 22

Scanning Lines

Very faint holographic scan.

Appears occasionally.

------------------------------------

LAYER 23

Ambient Fog

Very soft.

Slow movement.

------------------------------------

LAYER 24

Interaction Layer

Mouse movement.

Phone tilt.

Very slight parallax.

------------------------------------

LAYER 25

Speech Layer

Lip sync.

Jaw movement.

Eye contact.

Every layer should animate independently.

Nothing should be baked into one image.

Every feature should have its own component.

The Sentinel should feel assembled from multiple holographic systems rather than drawn as a single face.
