----------------------------------------------------
PART 4 — CONTRACT AND ROLL POLICY
----------------------------------------------------

Define a deterministic futures roll policy.

The policy must specify:

- which contract is active
- when the system rolls
- whether roll is volume-based, calendar-based, or expiration-based
- how overlapping contracts are handled
- whether price adjustment is applied
- whether raw and adjusted prices are both retained
- how duplicate timestamps at contract transitions are handled

Preferred initial policy:

- preserve raw contract data
- create a separate continuous research series
- retain contract identifier on every bar
- do not back-adjust prices unless explicitly supported
- avoid mixing two contracts at the same timestamp

Do not alter live instrument routing.
