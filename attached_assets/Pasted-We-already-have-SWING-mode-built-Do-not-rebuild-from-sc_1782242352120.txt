Required SWING mode changes:

1. Disable scalp logic inside SWING mode
SWING mode should not use:
- 1-minute entries
- tiny TP targets
- instant break-even movement
- overactive exit logic
- scalp-style chop exits

2. Use higher timeframe confirmation
SWING mode must prioritize:
- 1H trend
- 4H trend
- Daily key levels
- major supply/demand
- major liquidity zones

3. Swing entry rules
Allow entries only when:
- 1H and 4H bias agree
- price pulls back into a valid zone
- structure supports the direction
- there is at least 1:2 reward-to-risk available
- stop can be placed beyond structure or ATR level

4. Swing trade management
Once in a SWING trade, analyze every 15 minutes but do not overreact to every candle.

Each review should decide:
- HOLD
- REDUCE
- MOVE STOP
- EXIT

Do not exit just because a 1m or 5m candle goes against the trade.

5. Thesis tracking
Every SWING trade needs:
- original trade thesis
- current thesis status: VALID / WEAKENING / INVALID
- reason for status
- invalidation level
- next target
- next review time

6. Stop rules
Initial stop must be based on:
- structure invalidation
OR
- ATR swing stop
OR
- key level break

Do not use tight scalp stops in SWING mode.

7. Break-even rule
Do not move stop to break-even early.
For SWING:

- TP1 is hit
OR
- price reaches at least 1R
OR
- higher timeframe structure confirms continuation.
