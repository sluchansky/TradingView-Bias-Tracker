====================================================
PART 1 — VWAP SOURCE-AUTHORITY CORRECTION
====================================================

OBJECTIVE

Ensure fresh Databento VWAP can never be blocked by an older or stale
TradingView/chart VWAP.

Databento is now the primary live market-data source.

Replace timer-only source precedence with freshness-aware source precedence.

Required behavior:

1. A fresh Databento VWAP must replace a stale TradingView/chart VWAP.

2. A stale TradingView/chart value must never block a fresh Databento value.

3. TradingView/chart VWAP may only supplement Databento when:

   - Databento has not initialized yet, or
   - Databento data is unavailable or stale.

4. During Databento startup warm-up, the system may temporarily use a fresh
   chart VWAP, but it must switch to Databento as soon as Databento has a valid
   fresh value.

5. Record the active VWAP source and age.

Required diagnostic fields:

vwap_value
vwap_source
vwap_age_ms
databento_vwap_available
databento_vwap_age_ms
chart_vwap_available
chart_vwap_age_ms
source_selection_reason

Example:

{
  "vwap_value": 23184.25,
  "vwap_source": "databento",
  "vwap_age_ms": 420,
  "source_selection_reason": "fresh_primary_source"
}

Do not change the mathematical VWAP calculation in this task.

====================================================
ARCHITECTURAL BOUNDARY
====================================================

The Left Brain owns Market Intelligence conceptually.

However, do not perform heavy analysis directly inside the Databento trade
callback.

Required separation:

Databento ingestion layer
    → receives trades
    → updates thread-safe live market state
    → builds bars
    → updates indicators

Left Brain Market Intelligence layer
    → consumes an immutable or safely copied market-state snapshot
    → classifies the market
    → creates the narrative
    → ranks playbooks
    → publishes observational output

Databento ingestion must remain non-blocking and lightweight.

Target architecture:

Databento
    ↓
Live Market State
    ↓
LEFT BRAIN
    ├── Data State
    └── Market Intelligence
            ↓
      Existing Strategy/Gate Path
            ↓
RIGHT BRAIN
      Active Trade Management

During Phase 1B, the arrow from Market Intelligence to the strategy/gate path
is informational only.

No scoring or gate influence is permitted.
