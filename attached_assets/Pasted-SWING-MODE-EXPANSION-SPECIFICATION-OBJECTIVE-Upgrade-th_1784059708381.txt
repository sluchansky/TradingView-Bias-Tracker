SWING MODE EXPANSION SPECIFICATION

OBJECTIVE

Upgrade the existing Swing mode to identify, score, manage, and track trades intended to last approximately 2–7 trading days.

Do not modify the existing Scalp mode logic.

Swing mode must use separate:

* Timeframes
* Indicator settings
* Scoring weights
* Entry requirements
* Stop-loss calculations
* Target calculations
* Trade-management rules
* Alert frequency
* Database fields
* Dashboard presentation

The system must never reuse scalp thresholds simply because Swing mode is selected.

⸻

1. SWING MODE TIMEFRAMES

Use the following timeframe hierarchy:

* Weekly: long-term market direction and major levels
* Daily: primary swing trend and trade thesis
* 4-hour: setup structure and entry zone
* 1-hour: entry confirmation only
* 15-minute: optional precision entry; never determine the main swing direction

The Daily chart is the primary decision timeframe.

The Weekly and Daily charts determine direction.

The 4-hour and 1-hour charts determine entry timing.

Do not allow the 1-minute or 5-minute chart to invalidate an otherwise valid swing setup.

⸻

2. SWING DIRECTION REQUIREMENTS

LONG BIAS

A long swing bias should generally require:

* Weekly structure bullish or neutral-to-bullish
* Daily structure bullish
* Daily price above the 20 EMA or reclaiming it
* Daily 20 EMA above the 50 EMA, or crossing upward
* Price above the 200 EMA unless marked as an early reversal setup
* Higher highs and higher lows on Daily or 4-hour
* No major resistance immediately above the entry

SHORT BIAS

A short swing bias should generally require:

* Weekly structure bearish or neutral-to-bearish
* Daily structure bearish
* Daily price below the 20 EMA or rejecting it
* Daily 20 EMA below the 50 EMA, or crossing downward
* Price below the 200 EMA unless marked as an early reversal setup
* Lower highs and lower lows on Daily or 4-hour
* No major support immediately below the entry

Do not require every condition to pass. Use the scoring model and hard-block rules below.

⸻

3. SETUP TYPES

Swing mode must classify each opportunity into one of these setup types:

A. Trend Pullback

Long example:

* Daily trend bullish
* Price pulls back toward the Daily 20 EMA, 50 EMA, support, demand, prior breakout, or value area
* Pullback volume decreases
* 4-hour or 1-hour bullish confirmation appears

Short logic is reversed.

This should be the preferred swing setup.

B. Breakout and Retest

Long example:

* Daily or 4-hour resistance breaks
* Breakout has acceptable volume and candle strength
* Price retests the former resistance
* Former resistance holds as support
* Entry occurs after confirmation

Do not enter only because a breakout candle occurred.

C. Range Expansion

Requirements:

* Clearly defined multi-session range
* Price closes outside the range
* Volatility begins expanding
* Adequate room exists before the next major level
* Risk/reward remains acceptable

D. Higher-Timeframe Reversal

Requirements:

* Price reaches major Weekly or Daily support/resistance
* Prior trend shows exhaustion
* Daily or 4-hour CHOCH occurs
* Momentum divergence or declining trend strength is present
* Confirmation occurs before entry

Reversal setups must require a higher score than trend-continuation setups.

E. Compression Breakout

Requirements:

* ATR or range contracts over multiple sessions
* Price forms a tight base, flag, triangle, or compression
* Breakout occurs in alignment with the higher-timeframe bias
* Volume or momentum expands with the breakout

⸻

4. SWING SCORE

Create a separate swingScore from 0–100.

Do not reuse the scalp edge score.

Score Categories

Higher-Timeframe Trend: 20 points

Evaluate:

* Weekly direction alignment
* Daily direction alignment
* Daily 20/50/200 EMA alignment
* Higher highs/higher lows or lower highs/lower lows
* Trend persistence

Market Structure: 15 points

Evaluate:

* Daily BOS
* 4-hour BOS
* CHOCH quality
* Fresh structure level
* Clear invalidation level
* No conflicting nearby structure

Entry Location: 15 points

Evaluate proximity to:

* Daily or 4-hour support/resistance
* Fresh supply or demand zone
* 20 EMA or 50 EMA
* Prior breakout level
* Value area, anchored VWAP, or volume-profile level
* Fibonacci retracement zone when applicable

Deduct points when price is extended or being chased.

Momentum and Trend Strength: 10 points

Evaluate:

* RSI positioning
* MACD direction
* ADX or other trend-strength measurement
* Rate of change
* Momentum alignment across Daily and 4-hour

Momentum should confirm the trade without requiring an overbought or oversold condition.

Volume and Participation: 10 points

Evaluate:

* Breakout volume
* Relative volume
* Volume expansion in the trade direction
* Pullback volume contraction
* Accumulation or distribution
* CVD or delta when reliable data is available

Relative Strength and Correlation: 10 points

For equities, evaluate:

* Performance relative to SPY or QQQ
* Sector strength
* Industry strength
* Whether the instrument leads or lags the market

For futures, evaluate:

* Related-market confirmation
* Dollar, yields, volatility, commodities, or index correlations when relevant
* Whether correlations support or conflict with the trade

Risk and Reward Quality: 10 points

Evaluate:

* Distance from entry to structural stop
* Distance to each target
* Room before major opposing structure
* Reward-to-risk ratio
* Position size feasibility

Volatility Quality: 5 points

Evaluate:

* Current ATR
* Baseline ATR
* ATR percentile
* Whether volatility is contracting, normal, expanding, or disorderly
* Whether the stop can survive normal price movement

Catalyst and Event Risk: 5 points

Evaluate:

* Earnings
* FOMC
* CPI
* PPI
* Employment reports
* Major economic releases
* Contract rollover
* Company announcements
* Sector-specific events

Catalysts can add or subtract points depending on whether they support the thesis or create unacceptable risk.

⸻

5. SCORE THRESHOLDS

Use these default thresholds:

* 85–100: HIGH CONVICTION
* 75–84: READY
* 65–74: WATCHING
* 50–64: WEAK
* Below 50: NO TRADE

Trend-continuation setup minimum for READY:

* swingScore >= 75

Higher-timeframe reversal minimum for READY:

* swingScore >= 82

Auto-submit should remain disabled unless all risk, event, and execution requirements are satisfied.

The score must never override a hard-block condition.

⸻

6. HARD-BLOCK CONDITIONS

Return NO TRADE when any of the following are true:

* Reward-to-risk ratio is below 2.0
* No clear structural invalidation level exists
* Weekly and Daily trends strongly conflict
* Entry is directly into major opposing structure
* Major support or resistance is less than approximately 1 ATR from entry
* Price is excessively extended from the Daily 20 EMA
* Required stop exceeds the maximum permitted account risk
* Data is missing, stale, or unreliable
* The proposed position violates prop-firm rules
* Liquidity is insufficient
* Bid/ask spread is unacceptable for the instrument
* A major scheduled event creates unacceptable overnight risk
* Contract expiration or rollover creates abnormal risk
* The setup zone has already been fully mitigated
* The intended trade is based entirely on a 1-minute, 5-minute, or 15-minute signal

For individual stocks, block new entries shortly before earnings unless the user explicitly enables earnings-risk trades.

⸻

7. EXTENSION AND CHASE PROTECTION

Create the following fields:

* distanceFromDaily20EMA
* distanceFromDaily50EMA
* distanceFromEntryZone
* dailyATR
* extensionInATR

Calculate:

extensionInATR = absolute(price - daily20EMA) / dailyATR

Default interpretation:

* 0.00–0.75 ATR: healthy
* 0.76–1.25 ATR: elevated
* 1.26–1.75 ATR: extended
* Above 1.75 ATR: do not chase

An extended setup may remain in WATCHING status while waiting for a pullback.

⸻

8. ENTRY CONFIRMATION

A swing trade should not become READY based only on directional bias.

Require at least two entry confirmations from the following:

* 4-hour rejection candle
* 1-hour rejection candle
* 4-hour or 1-hour BOS in the trade direction
* CHOCH followed by confirmation
* Breakout and successful retest
* EMA reclaim or rejection
* Support or resistance hold
* Volume expansion
* Momentum crossover
* CVD or delta confirmation
* Liquidity sweep followed by reclaim
* Close back inside or outside a key level
* Higher low for long trades
* Lower high for short trades

At least one confirmation must come from price structure or price action.

Indicator-only confirmation is not sufficient.

⸻

9. ENTRY METHOD

Swing mode must output an entry zone instead of only one exact price.

Required fields:

* entryZoneLow
* entryZoneHigh
* preferredEntry
* entryMethod
* entryExpiration
* doNotChaseAbove
* doNotChaseBelow

Entry methods:

* Limit at pullback zone
* Stop entry after breakout confirmation
* Market entry after confirmed close
* Staged entry
* Retest entry

The system must explain what must happen before entry.

Example:

“Enter long only after a 1-hour close above 21,420 followed by a successful retest. Do not chase above 21,510.”

⸻

10. STOP-LOSS LOGIC

Swing stops must be structural and volatility-aware.

Do not use scalp-size fixed stops.

Stop placement order:

1. Beyond the setup invalidation level
2. Beyond the relevant Daily or 4-hour swing high/low
3. Beyond the supply/demand zone
4. Add an ATR buffer when necessary

Create:

* structuralStop
* atrStop
* recommendedStop
* stopDistance
* stopDistanceATR
* invalidationReason

Default ATR buffer:

* Approximately 0.20–0.40 of Daily ATR beyond the structural level

Do not widen the stop merely to achieve a preferred position size.

Reduce position size to match the proper stop.

⸻

11. TARGET LOGIC

Targets must be based on structure rather than arbitrary percentages.

Create:

* target1
* target2
* target3
* runnerTarget
* target1Reason
* target2Reason
* target3Reason
* rewardRiskTarget1
* rewardRiskTarget2
* rewardRiskTarget3

Target hierarchy:

* Prior Daily or 4-hour high/low
* Major supply/demand zone
* Weekly support/resistance
* Volume-profile level
* Measured move
* ATR expansion target
* Fibonacci extension when applicable

Minimum trade requirement:

* At least one realistic target must offer 2R or greater.

Preferred structure:

* TP1: approximately 1.5R–2R
* TP2: approximately 2.5R–3.5R
* TP3 or runner: 4R or higher when structure allows

Do not invent a 5R target when major structure makes it unrealistic.

⸻

12. POSITION SIZING

Position size must be calculated from the actual structural stop.

Required inputs:

* Account balance
* Maximum risk percentage
* Entry price
* Stop price
* Point or tick value
* Contract or share increment
* Existing portfolio exposure
* Correlated exposure
* Prop-firm limits

Required outputs:

* riskDollars
* riskPercent
* positionSize
* positionValue
* maximumLoss
* correlatedRisk
* portfolioHeatAfterEntry

Recommended default swing risk:

* 0.25%–0.50% per trade for prop accounts
* Never automatically exceed the user’s configured risk
* Reduce risk when holding through major scheduled events
* Reduce risk when multiple open positions are highly correlated

14. DAILY TRADE MANAGEMENT

Once a swing trade is entered, reassess it after each:

* Daily close
* 4-hour close
* Major event
* Target hit
* Stop adjustment
* Material structure change

Do not continuously reverse the thesis because of minor intraday fluctuations.

At each reassessment, output:

* Thesis intact: yes/no
* Trend intact: yes/no
* Structure intact: yes/no
* Momentum improving/stable/weakening
* Volume supportive/neutral/conflicting
* Event risk
* Current unrealized R
* Recommended action

Recommended actions:

* HOLD
* HOLD WITH CAUTION
* TAKE PARTIAL
* MOVE STOP
* TRAIL STOP
* DO NOT ADD
* EXIT
* THESIS INVALIDATED

⸻

15. STOP MANAGEMENT

Default management rules:

Before TP1

* Do not move the stop to breakeven simply because price briefly moves in profit.
* Keep the structural stop unless the thesis changes.
* Avoid choking a valid swing trade with intraday noise.

At TP1

The system may:

* Take 25%–50% partial profit
* Move stop only when new structure supports the move
* Move stop below a new higher low for longs
* Move stop above a new lower high for shorts

After TP2

The system may:

* Take another partial
* Trail using Daily or 4-hour structure
* Trail using the 20 EMA
* Trail using an ATR-based stop
* Preserve a runner when the trend remains strong

Every stop adjustment must include a structural reason.

⸻

16. EXIT CONDITIONS

Generate an EXIT or EXIT WARNING when:

* Daily thesis is invalidated
* Opposite Daily BOS occurs
* Key support/resistance decisively fails
* Price closes beyond the structural stop
* Momentum and structure both reverse
* A major gap invalidates the expected risk
* Event risk becomes unacceptable
* Target structure is reached and trend weakens
* Trade duration expires without expected follow-through
* Relative strength meaningfully deteriorates
* Volume shows clear distribution in a long or accumulation in a short

Do not exit solely because of one weak 15-minute candle.

⸻

17. TIME-BASED EXPIRATION

Create:

* setupCreatedAt
* entryValidUntil
* expectedHoldMinDays
* expectedHoldMaxDays
* daysInTrade
* timeStopDate

Default rules:

* Untriggered entry setup expires after 3–5 trading sessions unless refreshed
* Breakout setup expires if price runs beyond the chase threshold
* Entered trade receives a time-stop review if no meaningful progress occurs after 3 trading sessions
* Standard expected holding period is 2–7 trading days
* Trades may remain open longer only if trend, structure, and risk management support it

⸻

19. MARKET REGIME

Create a regime classification:

* STRONG BULL TREND
* BULL TREND
* NEUTRAL/RANGE
* BEAR TREND
* STRONG BEAR TREND
* HIGH-VOLATILITY RISK-OFF
* LOW-VOLATILITY COMPRESSION
* TRANSITION/UNCERTAIN

Swing mode should favor:

* Longs during bullish regimes
* Shorts during bearish regimes
* Smaller size or no trade during uncertain regimes
* Breakout setups during compression
* Mean-reversion setups only when explicitly enabled

Market regime should modify the score but should not fabricate certainty.

23. ALERT BEHAVIOR

Swing alerts should fire only for meaningful status changes.

Send an alert when:

* New setup reaches WATCHING
* Setup becomes READY
* Entry conditions trigger
* Setup becomes extended
* Setup is invalidated
* Entry expires
* TP1, TP2, or TP3 is reached
* Stop adjustment is recommended
* Exit warning occurs
* Thesis becomes invalid
* Overnight or weekend risk materially changes

Do not send alerts every minute while the same condition remains true.

Deduplicate alerts using:

* Symbol
* Setup ID
* Lifecycle status
* Direction
* Relevant price level

⸻

24. DATABASE SEPARATION

Store swing setups separately from scalp setups or include a mandatory tradeMode field.

Required values:

* SCALP
* SWING

Every setup, alert, trade, score, journal entry, and performance record must include tradeMode.

Swing performance must be measured separately from scalp performance.

Track:

* Win rate
* Average R
* Median R
* Average holding period
* Maximum adverse excursion
* Maximum favorable excursion
* Profit factor
* Setup-type performance
* Regime performance
* Long versus short performance
* Time-to-target
* Exit efficiency
* Number of unnecessary early exits

25. SAFETY AND DEPLOYMENT

Do not immediately enable automated swing execution.

Deployment order:

1. Implement analysis fields
2. Implement score
3. Implement dashboard
4. Implement alerts
5. Run historical backtests
6. Run walk-forward testing
7. Run paper trading
8. Compare expected versus actual performance
9. Enable manual approval
10. Consider automation only after validated results

Add a feature flag:

SWING_MODE_V2_ENABLED

Add another independent feature flag:

SWING_AUTO_EXECUTION_ENABLED

Default:

* Swing Mode V2: enabled after testing
* Swing auto-execution: false

Do not change existing Scalp mode files, thresholds, or behavior unless shared infrastructure requires a backward-compatible update.
