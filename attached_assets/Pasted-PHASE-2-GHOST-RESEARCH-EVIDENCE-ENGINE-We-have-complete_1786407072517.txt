PHASE 2 — GHOST RESEARCH & EVIDENCE ENGINE

We have completed the Canonical Databento Market State foundation.

The next objective is to turn the existing ghost/research infrastructure into a rigorous experimentation and evidence system.

THIS PHASE MUST NOT CHANGE LIVE TRADING BEHAVIOR.

Do not alter live strategy thresholds, scoring, READY/EARLY/WAIT, risk, execution, TradersPost, prop protection, arm-state behavior, ORB live behavior, or broker orders.

The live system remains conservative.

The ghost system becomes the experimental laboratory.

==================================================
16. HUMAN APPROVAL REQUIRED
==================================================

READY_FOR_REVIEW does NOT mean live.

change live configuration
change strategy weights

change gates

change execution

Promotion into live behavior requires explicit operator approval in a later phase.

==================================================
17. FORWARD VALIDATION
==================================================

When a candidate reaches the appropriate research threshold:

freeze its configuration.

Create a versioned forward-validation candidate.

Once frozen:

do not optimize it using forward-validation data.

Track forward results separately.

This prevents repeated tuning to the test set.

==================================================
18. PARAMETER STABILITY
==================================================

Eventually the system must distinguish:

real edge

from

one lucky parameter value.

For numerical parameters, compare nearby values.

Example:

ORB breakout buffer:

2 ticks
3 ticks
4 ticks
5 ticks
6 ticks

A strategy that only works at exactly 4 ticks but collapses at 3 and 5 should receive a stability warning.

Do this within reasonable experiment budgets.
