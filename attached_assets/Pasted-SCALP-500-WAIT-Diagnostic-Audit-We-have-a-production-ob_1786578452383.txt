SCALP 500-WAIT Diagnostic Audit

We have a production observation from the SCALP mode:

* Setups analyzed: 500
* READY: 0
* WAIT: 500
* Databento attribution: 49.1%
* TradingView: 14.8%
* Internal: 14.3%
* Unknown: 21.8%
* Buying-pressure duplicate evidence: 95.4%
* CVD + Volume co-occurrence: 100%
* Sweep + CVD: 100%
* Sweep + Volume: 100%
* VWAP + CVD: 100%
* VWAP + Volume: 100%
* Average evidence ages:
    * BOS: 2115.2 sec
    * CHOCH: 403.7 sec
    * Sweep: 407.9 sec
    * CVD: 30.2 sec
    * VWAP: 30.2 sec
    * Volume: 30.1 sec

Objective

Determine why SCALP produced 0 READY setups out of 500 and whether this represents:

1. legitimately poor market conditions,
2. an overly restrictive SCALP gate,
3. stale structural evidence,
4. duplicated/correlated evidence,
5. incorrectly populated/defaulted confirmations,
6. a data-source problem,
7. or a logging/analytics problem.

HARD CONSTRAINTS

Do not change trading behavior during the initial audit.

Do not:

* lower READY thresholds
* increase/decrease edge-score weights
* remove gates
* change risk
* change stops or targets
* modify SCALP execution
* modify SWING
* modify INTRADAY_TREND
* modify ORB
* add indicators
* create a new strategy
* change production behavior merely to generate more trades

This is a diagnostic audit first.
