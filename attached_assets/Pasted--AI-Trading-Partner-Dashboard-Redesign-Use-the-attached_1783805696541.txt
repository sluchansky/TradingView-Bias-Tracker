# AI Trading Partner Dashboard Redesign

Use the attached reference image as the primary design target.

Do NOT simply reorganize the existing dashboard. This should be a complete frontend redesign while preserving all existing backend functionality, APIs, Brain logic, TradersPost integration, Discord integration, chart data, and trading engine.

The goal is to make the application feel like a premium AI trading command center rather than a collection of widgets.

--------------------------------------------------
PRIMARY GOAL
--------------------------------------------------

The AI Trading Partner should feel like a professional analyst sitting beside me.

The dashboard should revolve around a realistic AI avatar that continuously explains what it is seeing, why it is waiting, and why it recommends trades.

The avatar should become the centerpiece of the application.

--------------------------------------------------
REMOVE
--------------------------------------------------

Completely remove:

• Particle face
• Dot matrix face
• Wireframe face
• Floating blue dots
• Robotic mask
• Cartoon appearance
• Uncanny AI face

Replace it with a realistic cinematic AI analyst.

--------------------------------------------------
AVATAR REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------

The avatar should be:

• Realistic human
• Male
• Professional
• Calm
• Intelligent
• High quality
• Symmetrical
• Slight blue cinematic lighting
• Looking directly toward the user
• Chest-up framing
• Premium AAA quality

Animation should include only:

• Blinking
• Small eye movement
• Subtle breathing
• Small head movement
• Mouth movement while speaking
• Lighting reacts to confidence level

The avatar should never feel distracting.
