TASK: Phase 3B — Simplify Home Dashboard Around Brain Contract

OBJECTIVE

Simplify the Home dashboard so it presents one clear operator view of the
canonical data.brain contract.

This phase is a visual and information-architecture cleanup only.

Do not change:

- backend analysis
- brain contract schema
- scoring
- thresholds
- gates
- execution
- sizing
- alerts
- database behavior
- strategy behavior
- Cockpit data logic
- Databento integration

CORE RULE

Home is the operator summary.

Cockpit is the detailed diagnostic view.

Home must show only the information needed to answer:

1. What instrument am I looking at?
2. Is the data fresh?
3. What is the Brain’s verdict?
4. What is the primary edge score?
5. What direction is supported?
6. Why?
7. What are the risks?
8. What should happen next?
9. Is there a valid trade plan?
