# 4. PURPOSE OF INTRADAY TREND

The strategy has one primary job:

> Detect and participate in the major directional MNQ move occurring during the current trading day.

This is NOT a scalp strategy.

We are looking for approximately:

* 100-point moves
* 150-point moves
* 200-point moves
* 300+ point expansion days

Not every trade needs to travel 100 points.

However, the setup must have enough structural room that a large move is realistically available.

The strategy should prefer:

**fewer high-quality trades over frequent signals.**

Default maximum:

`MAX_INTRADAY_TREND_TRADES_PER_DAY = 2`
