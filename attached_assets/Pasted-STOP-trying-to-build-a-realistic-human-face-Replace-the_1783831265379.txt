DESIGN GOALS

The Sentinel is not a human.

The Sentinel is not a robot.

The Sentinel is an advanced artificial intelligence represented by a mysterious, premium holographic guardian.

The overall feeling should be:

- Calm
- Intelligent
- Confident
- Mysterious
- Professional
- Futuristic

Never creepy.
Never horror themed.
Never cartoonish.

--------------------------------------------------

HEAD DESIGN

Create a smooth matte black mask.

The surface should be clean and minimal.

No skin.

No wrinkles.

No realistic facial anatomy.

The face should feel sculpted from a futuristic material.

Use soft curves.

Avoid sharp edges.

The mask should have subtle blue reflections that react to movement.

--------------------------------------------------

EYES

The eyes are the most important feature.

Requirements:

- Bright electric blue irises
- Deep pupils
- Soft glowing eye rings
- Multiple reflections
- Smooth blinking
- Natural eye movement
- Eyes follow important dashboard events
- Eyes maintain eye contact while speaking
- Pupil size changes slightly while thinking

The eyes should communicate almost all emotion.

--------------------------------------------------

MOUTH

The mouth should be very minimal.

A thin illuminated line when idle.

When speaking:

- Smooth lip animation
- Small jaw movement
- Natural speech timing
- No exaggerated expressions

The mouth should disappear back into the mask when silent.

--------------------------------------------------

NOSE

Do not build a realistic nose.

Only hint at the bridge using lighting.

No nostrils.

No outlines.

--------------------------------------------------

HEAD SHAPE

Smooth oval shape.

Slightly elongated.

Premium proportions.

No ears.

No hair.

No beard.

No facial texture.

--------------------------------------------------

OUTER DESIGN

Instead of showing a neck, blend the lower face into a dark hood or high collar made of animated holographic energy.

The hood should feel like energy instead of cloth.

Soft moving particles should surround the shoulders.

--------------------------------------------------

LIGHTING

Use cinematic lighting.

Primary blue light.

Very soft secondary purple rim light.

Gentle ambient glow.

Subtle reflections across the mask.

No harsh highlights.

--------------------------------------------------

PARTICLES

Small floating particles should orbit around the avatar.

Occasional flowing energy lines.

Slow movement.

Nothing distracting.

--------------------------------------------------

IDLE ANIMATION

While idle:

Slow breathing glow

Blink every few seconds

Tiny eye adjustments

Very slight head movement

Soft pulsing light

Occasional particle movement

The avatar should never appear frozen.

--------------------------------------------------

ANALYZING

Eyes move around the dashboard.

Glow increases.

Particles move faster.

Small head movements.

Confidence ring slowly rotates.

--------------------------------------------------

READY

Eyes brighten.

Glow becomes stronger.

Head lifts slightly.

Looks directly at the user.

--------------------------------------------------

SPEAKING

Eyes stay focused.

Natural blinking.

Mouth animates using speech timing.

Jaw moves subtly.

Soft pulse around the face.
