TASK: Phase 6B.1C — Strategy Eligibility Engine (Read-Only Shadow Mode)

OBJECTIVE

Build a Strategy Eligibility Engine that evaluates whether each strategy
SHOULD be allowed to participate under current market conditions.

This phase is SHADOW MODE ONLY.

DO NOT change trade execution.

DO NOT change scoring.

DO NOT block trades.

DO NOT modify detectors.

DO NOT modify learning.

DO NOT modify TradersPost.

DO NOT modify Databento.

DO NOT regenerate the historical baseline.

The engine will only produce diagnostics.

----------------------------------------------------
BACKGROUND
----------------------------------------------------

Phase 6B.1B showed:

- ORB Long strongly positive while ORB Short struggled during this dataset.
- LSR performed well during volatile conditions and poorly in quiet sessions.
- Opening Drive performed best in established trends.
- VWAP Trend Continuation deteriorated during overnight sessions.
- Range Expansion Breakout varied significantly by regime.

Rather than changing strategies, build a reusable eligibility layer that
explains whether market conditions favor each strategy.

----------------------------------------------------
PART 1 — STRATEGY ELIGIBILITY MODEL
----------------------------------------------------

For every strategy create a diagnostic eligibility object.

Return:

eligible: true/false
confidence: 0–100
reasons: []
warnings: []

The object must never affect execution.

----------------------------------------------------
PART 2 — INPUTS
----------------------------------------------------

Evaluate using existing information only.

Include where available:

- session
- ET hour
- volatility regime
- trend regime
- VWAP relationship
- opening-range status
- ATR regime
- day of week
- instrument
- mode
- higher timeframe bias if already available

Do not create new detectors.

----------------------------------------------------
PART 3 — STRATEGY PROFILES
----------------------------------------------------

Create diagnostic profiles for:

Opening Drive

Liquidity Sweep Reversal

VWAP Trend Continuation

Range Expansion Breakout

Opening Range Breakout

Each profile should describe:

- preferred session
- weak session
- preferred volatility
- weak volatility
- preferred trend
- weak trend
- preferred directionality
- historical confidence

These are diagnostics only.
